Insurance Act No. 4 of 2018 — Schedule 11. Standards of Market Conduct B. Understanding Consumers' Needs. The insurance intermediary or officer should be required to obtain sufficient information about the consumer to ensure an appropriate product is offered. Formal "fact finds" should be specified for long-term savings and investment products and they should be retained and be available for inspection for a reasonable number of years.

Market Conduct Guideline (Insurance Act 2018). The sale of products and services should be based on what is best suited for the consumer, given their needs, financial capability, risk profile and specific circumstances.

Decision-support disclaimer
RAI is analytical decision-support for the licensed advisor. It does not advise clients, does not select or sell products, and does not replace the advisor’s professional judgment or the client’s free choice. Analysis categories are indicative only — a given need may properly be met by more than one category of product. Every recommendation, and the written reason for it, is made and owned by the advisor and reviewed by their direct manager, in keeping with the Insurance Act No. 4 of 2018 (Schedule 11 — Standards of Market Conduct) and the Market Conduct Guideline for Registrants.
