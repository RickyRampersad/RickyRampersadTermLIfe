# The RR Branch video standard

Everything needed to make the next video sound and look like this one. Use it
verbatim — the point is that a run of videos feels like one series, not five
different attempts.

---

## Voice

| | |
|---|---|
| **Engine** | `edge-tts` (Microsoft Edge neural text-to-speech) |
| **Voice ID** | `en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural` |
| **Rate** | `-3%` |
| **Pitch / volume** | default — do not touch |

```bash
edge-tts -v en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural --rate=-3% \
  -t "your line here" \
  --write-media line.mp3 --write-subtitles line.vtt
```

This is Andrew's *expressive* model, not the base one. The base `en-US-AndrewNeural`
reads a script accurately but flat; the multilingual model carries the sentence.
At `-3%` it lands just under conversational pace, which is where narration wants
to sit.

Always write `--write-subtitles`. The VTT carries real millisecond word
boundaries, and those drive the caption timing. Never guess caption timing.

### Writing for it

The voice is only half of "natural" — the script is the other half.

- **Use contractions.** "It is one form" reads robotic; "It's one form" doesn't.
- **Short clauses, full stops.** "Type the income. Type the expenses." beats one
  long sentence with commas.
- **No jargon.** Say "the money the family would have to find", not "the
  quantified protection shortfall".
- **Spell out figures the way a person says them**: "nine hundred and sixty
  thousand", not "960,000". The engine reads digits mechanically.
- **Spell out initialisms with spaces** so they are read as letters:
  `M D R T`, not `MDRT`.

## Music

There is no licensed track and nothing to attribute. The bed is generated by
`music_gen.py` in this folder.

**Name it the *factfind360 Theme*** when anyone asks.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Key** | D major |
| **Progression** | I – V – vi – IV (D · A · Bm · G) |
| **Tempo** | 108 BPM |
| **Parts** | warm detuned pads, plucked eighth-note arpeggio, brushed offbeat hat, bass pulse on the beat |
| **Master** | one-pole lowpass at 3.4 kHz, 2s fade in, 4s fade out, slight Haas width |

```bash
python3 music_gen.py <seconds> output.wav
```

Render it to the exact length of the finished cut, plus one second.

### Mixing it under the narration

Side-chain the music off the voice so it lifts between sentences and drops
under them. Do not just turn it down — a static bed either fights the voice or
disappears.

```
[0:a]dynaudnorm=f=250:g=7:p=0.9[voc];
[voc]asplit=2[voc1][key];
[1:a]volume=0.34[mus];
[mus][key]sidechaincompress=threshold=0.045:ratio=11:attack=15:release=420:makeup=1[duck];
[voc1][duck]amix=inputs=2:duration=first:dropout_transition=0:normalize=0,alimiter=limit=0.94[a]
```

Target **−14 LUFS integrated**. That is the streaming standard and it is loud
enough on a phone speaker.

## Picture

| | |
|---|---|
| **Frame** | 1280 × 720, 30 fps, H.264 High, yuv420p |
| **Page area** | 1280 × 624 — the page is recorded short |
| **Caption bar** | bottom 96px, its own strip |
| **Always** | `-movflags +faststart`, or the file will not start playing until it has fully downloaded |

Captions go **under** the page, never on top of it. A subtitle drawn over a
dashboard covers the number it is describing.

### Palette

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Ground | `#0E1A2E` |
| Panel | `#0D1524` |
| Ink | `#F3F7FD` |
| Muted ink | `#93A7C4` |
| **Teal — figures, money, counts** | `#2DD4BF` |
| **Gold — decisions, status words** | `#F5B935` |
| Violet — held / parked | `#A78BFA` |
| Red — overdue | `#FB7185` |

In captions, money and counts go teal; *approved*, *declined*, *sent back*,
*live*, *instantly* go gold. Everything else stays white.

## Sample data and the disclaimer

**Never put a real client on screen.** Every frame showing client data carries
a stamp in the top-right corner for its whole duration:

> ● SAMPLE DATA · NOT A REAL CLIENT

The demonstration household lives in `mkvid.js` as `DEMO` — Marcus and Denise
Alleyne, two children, a dependent parent. Deliberately unmistakable as sample
data: the email is on the reserved `example.com` domain, the ID number is a run
of zeroes, the employers are "Sample Employer Ltd" and "Sample Health Ltd".

The narration says it out loud too, the first time the form appears.

### Product names

Only ever use plan names from the fact find's own catalogue — Flexi Term,
Econolife, Liberator, Reliance Plan, Rejuvenator, Lifecare Quantum, Provisor,
Tophat, Life Secure, SPIA, IPI. Read them straight out of the `rec1Rec`
dropdown in `FFPROJECT.html`.

An invented product name on screen is the fastest way to lose a room of people
who sell the real ones.

Every cut opens and closes on the same two facts:

- **Created by Ricky Rampersad, Branch Manager** — conceived, designed and
  driven from inside the Ricky Rampersad Branch
- **35 years in the field · Guardian Life of the Caribbean**

The credentials, stated in full. "MDRT member" undersells this badly:

| Credential | Say it as |
|---|---|
| **MDRT Life Member** | Million Dollar Round Table |
| **Top of the Table — four times** | MDRT's highest level · **six times** the qualifying requirement |
| **Court of the Table — nine years** | **three times** the qualifying requirement · most recently last year |
| **MFA, LUTCF** | Life Underwriter Training Council Fellow |
| **33 years with Guardian** | 35 in the industry · two at ALGICO before that |

Always spell out the multiple. Outside the MDRT world "Top of the Table" means
nothing; "six times what MDRT itself asks for" means everything. Top of the
Table is the highest level MDRT awards, Court of the Table the one below it.

**The career, not just a tenure.** Thirty-five years in the industry: two at
ALGICO — American Life and General Insurance Company (Trinidad & Tobago)
Limited, an AIG member company — where he started, and thirty-three since with
Guardian Life of the Caribbean. Say it that way round; "35 years" alone loses
the fact that thirty-three of them are with the company the audience works for.

Thirteen of those years were spent qualifying at Court of the Table or above,
the most recent last year. That sentence is what turns a list of badges into an
argument: the judgement in the system is neither theoretical nor historical.

**Provenance.** MFA and LUTCF are corroborated in his own 2023 email signature
and on his about.me profile. The MDRT counts and the career split come from him
directly — they are on no public page and in no mail in the account — so
anything going to Guardian executives should travel with the certificates. His
public profiles also still say "20+ years" against a true thirty-five.

## The pipeline

```bash
node mktts.js     # narration + subtitle timing   → vid/nvoice/
node mkcaps.js    # one caption image per sentence → vid/caps/
node mkvid.js     # record every scene            → vid/rec/
node mkseg.js     # letterbox + captions + audio  → vid/seg/
node mkfilm.js    # three cuts, music, mastering
```

`ONLY=S12,S16` on any of them restricts the run to those scenes.

Three cuts come out of it every time:

| Cut | Use |
|---|---|
| `factfind360-tour.mp4` | the site, presentations |
| `tour_60s.mp4` | the highlight, for a quick share |
| `tour_whatsapp.mp4` | 960×540, under 16MB, travels on WhatsApp |

### Version the filename every single time

Ship the site copy as `factfind360-tour-vN.mp4` and the poster as
`tour-poster-vN.jpg`, bumping N on every cut, and update the two references in
`walkthrough.html` and `index.html` to match.

A video re-published under a filename a browser has already seen is served from
cache, not from the server — so the site keeps showing the previous cut with no
sign anything is wrong. The same collision happens in a Downloads folder, where
ten zips all extract to the same name. A new filename cannot be served from a
cache that has never seen it.

Sanity check for whoever is looking at a copy and is not sure which cut it is:
read the duration. Each version is a different length.
