Trinidad & Tobago

Where you stand under local law

Your rights don't depend on your agent turning up. They're written into the Insurance Act, 2018.

Your policy is with the company

Not with an individual. When an agent leaves, the insurer's obligations — collecting premiums, servicing the policy, paying claims — carry on exactly as before.

Money can sit unclaimed for years

Under section 197 of the Insurance Act, 2018, sums payable but unclaimed for seven years after maturity are reported to the Central Bank as unclaimed monies. A matured policy nobody told you about is exactly how that happens.

You may appoint who services you

Choosing a servicing agent is your decision, and it changes nothing about your cover, premium or benefits. If you'd rather stay with the company directly, that's a perfectly good answer too.

Before you start

The questions you're actually thinking

Including the awkward ones.

What if I don't know my policy number?
Then don't give us one. Most people don't have it, and the form never requires it — that's a deliberate design decision, not an oversight. Your name, date of birth and a contact number are enough for us to trace a policy. Not knowing what you own is the normal condition, not an embarrassing one: only 15% of policyholders say they completely understand their policy.
Is this really free? What's the catch?
It's free, and the catch is the honest one: we're an agency, and a proportion of people we help will eventually ask us to do more. That's the whole business model. What we don't do is make the review conditional on it. You can complete this, take your Policy Location Record, get your records fixed, and never speak to us again. That happens regularly and it's fine.
Will this change my policy or my premium?
No. A servicing agent is who looks after you, not what you own. Your cover, premium, benefits, maturity date and every guarantee in your contract are untouched. If you also ask us to change something — a beneficiary, an address, how you pay — that's a separate, explicit request, and the insurer confirms it to you in writing before it takes effect.
Are you going to try to sell me a new policy?
If you came here on your own, the product questions are switched off entirely — the form doesn't ask what you'd like to buy, only what you'd like help with. If your agent sent you the link, the coverage questions are included, because a relationship already exists and a review without them isn't much of a review. Either way: we will never advise you to cancel or replace a policy that's doing its job. Old policies are frequently better than what's sold today.
Nobody has called me in years. Why should I trust this?
That's fair, and we won't argue you out of it. The industry earned that. What we'd say is: judge it by what happens next rather than by what we claim here. You'll get a written confirmation with a reference number, a named person and a response time. If we miss it, you have it in writing — and our own system chases us when we're late.
What happens to my information?
It goes to our office to service your policy and to the insurer's customer service department for anything that needs their action — a record correction, a change of servicing agent. Nowhere else. We don't ask for ID numbers, bank details or medical history, and we don't sell data. Marketing consent is a separate, optional tick that changes nothing about your service if you decline.
My agent died, or I genuinely don't know who they were.
This is more common than you'd think, and it's exactly who this is for. Start the review, say so in the "what would you like help with" box, and we'll trace the policy from your name and date of birth. If you're a family member who's found paperwork for someone who has passed away, say that too — we'll help you either way, and there's no charge for looking.
I'm an agent. Can I use this for my own clients?
Yes — that's the other half of the product. The agent console generates a personal, prefilled link for each client and a ready-to-send message. Reviews come back tagged to you, with the coverage questions switched on.
