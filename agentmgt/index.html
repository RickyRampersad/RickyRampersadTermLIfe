Who this is for

Two kinds of people walk through this door.

Different fears, the same five gates. Here is what each one is actually worried about, and what is true in this branch.

Already contracted somewhere else

  • “If I move, I start from zero.”

    Clients whose agent has moved on are matched to you, household by household, with the file, the history and the first thing to say. Each one whose service questionnaire comes in after your naming pays you TT$200.

  • “I'd be buying my own leads again.”

    Your own link and QR code. A prospect taps what is on their mind and the lead lands on your board, in their words. A lead forwarded to you stays yours.

  • “The tools are from another decade.”

    A fact find that does the arithmetic while you talk. A production wall that is live on the branch screen and on your phone. Branch Intelligence that reads the file before you do.

  • “Nobody would notice if I doubled my year.”

    Wins are announced every Wednesday in the branch meeting, at Blastoff in January and at Mid-Year in July. Your name, out loud.

  • “Word gets back.”

    Your conversation with us stays confidential. At your Discovery you sit with our qualifiers and ask them anything, and the Branch Manager leaves the room.

New to insurance

  • “I can't go months with nothing coming in.”

    It is commission-based and we will not dress that up. What shortens the start is here from day one: leads, matched households, a day the system sets, and a manager who sees your week before Friday. At your conversation you see the branch's own figures, not an adjective.

  • “I'd have to sell to my friends and family.”

    You don't live off your phone contacts. The leads come to you, from people who asked to be called.

  • “They promise training and then you're on your own.”

    Training, induction, boot camp and one-on-one coaching. The Branch Manager interviews every advisor personally and coaches you after.

  • “I wouldn't know what to do each morning.”

    Every morning the system sets your day: your projection, your next calls, your pending cases with the history on them. Check in, and your workday page shows what you have achieved as you go.

  • “I'm leaving a salary for a ceiling I can't see.”

    There is no salary band and no ceiling. The ladder is written down, rung by rung, and you choose which one you climb.

What this career pays, honestly

No salary band. No ceiling. No adjective in place of a number.

How it is paid

Commission on what you write, uncapped, from day one. TT$200 for every matched household whose service questionnaire comes in after you are named, chased by the system until it is paid. Overrides and allowances at the management rungs. No salary band, so nothing holds the top down.

What grows it

Activity times skill. The leads programme and the matched households shorten the start; the FY27 flight plan works from what your life costs back to the API that pays for it, in fifteen minutes.

What we won't print

An average we made up. At your conversation you see the branch's own record, year by year, and you bring your questions. Thirty-two years of producers are what that record shows.

The Selection

Five gates. Deliberately.

We select using the same science-based process for everyone — newcomer or fifteen-year producer. It’s rigorous because the room stays strong only if the door is.

  1. Apply & screen

    Two minutes online, smart screening questions. Confidential for practising advisors.

  2. Meet the Branch Manager

    A real conversation with Ricky Rampersad himself. Every advisor here started with it — no exceptions. Then an orientation: the career, the branch, the numbers, no gloss.

  3. Discovery sessions

    Structured job sampling, and your own digital market survey: the people you know, mapped before you start. And your chance to inspect us, our advisors and this environment from the inside.

  4. Scientific assessment

    POP7, the sales-potential profile the industry uses to predict who makes it. We select on evidence, not vibes — it protects you as much as us.

  5. Final selection

    Your file goes to executive review. Pass this, and you haven’t found a job — you’ve been chosen for a career.

Rigorous on purpose: an offer from this branch means something. The advisors beside you earned their seat the same way you will.

Straight Answers

The questions you’re already asking.

I’m already contracted at another branch or company. Why move?

Because production follows environment. If you’re carrying your numbers despite where you sit — no mentorship, no recognition, no plan for your next rank — then the ceiling is the room, not you. Here the path to Executive Agent, Senior Sales Consultant and management is written, coached and announced. Your conversation with us stays fully confidential.

I have no insurance experience. Is that a problem?

No — it’s normal. Newcomers get the full build: training, induction, boot camp, one-on-one coaching, and mentors who are required by their own appointments to develop you. What we can’t teach is drive, resilience and trustworthiness — that’s what the selection tests.

Is the income really uncapped?

Yes. Earnings are commission-based, so they scale with your activity and skill — no salary band, no ceiling, and at senior ranks, overriding commissions on top. It also means the first months take real work while you build. We’re upfront about both sides.

What’s the catch?

The standard. We select — we don’t fill seats. Five gates, including a scientific assessment and a personal interview with the Branch Manager, for newcomers and veterans alike. Most applicants don’t make it. The ones who do get a career, a coach, and a room that pulls them upward.

How fast does this move?

The application takes about 2 minutes and is reviewed within 48 hours. Clear the screen and you’ll meet the Branch Manager, then move into Discovery — weeks, not months, driven mostly by your own pace.
