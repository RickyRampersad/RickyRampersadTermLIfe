A new member’s own link

Make the link on the staff page, paste it into respond.io, and it goes the rest of the way: the employee fills in their part on their phone, HR checks and approves it, and it goes to Guardian Life with a task for our team.

    1. 1

      Make the link

      Staff page → the group → Links to send → Make a link for a new member. Their name, mobile, the date cover starts and the plans.

    2. 2

      Paste it into respond.io

      Press Copy WhatsApp message and paste it into their chat. The link is theirs alone and stays open for 60 days.

    3. 3

      Watch where they stand

      Waiting on them, ready for HR to check, or sent to Guardian Life. Our team’s task follows it up every three working days.
