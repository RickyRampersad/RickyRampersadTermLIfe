Sit a timed test

No answers until you submit, a clock that does not stop, and a full review at the end. This is the half of preparation that practice drills cannot give you.

CSEC Paper 01 gives you about 90 seconds a question. These tests use the same pace. Answer everything — there is no penalty for a wrong answer, so a guess on a question you cannot do is worth more than a blank. Flag anything you want to come back to.
