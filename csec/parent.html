How to help without hovering

Ask for the mistake, not the mark

"What did you get wrong today and why?" tells you far more than a percentage. Every question in the hub shows its reasoning, so the answer to that question is always available.

Protect the streak, not the hour

Ten questions a night, every night, beats three hours on a Sunday. The streak square on this page is the number to watch — gaps in it explain most falling marks.

Form 3 is the decision year

Subject choices for CSEC are made on Form 3 performance. A weak strand fixed in Form 2 costs an evening; the same gap in Form 4 costs a grade.

Download the syllabus

CXC publishes every CSEC syllabus free. It lists exactly what the examiner can ask. The Past papers page links each one.
