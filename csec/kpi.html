Read this before you read the numbers. The readiness index is built from practice inside this hub. It is not a CXC prediction and not a grade. Practice accuracy flatters — multiple choice, no time pressure, an explanation after every question — so retention and timed tests are weighted, and coverage caps the score, because nobody is ready for a paper they have seen a third of. Treat a band as a direction of travel, and treat school reports and real past papers as the truth.
