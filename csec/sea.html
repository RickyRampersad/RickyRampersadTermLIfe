SEA practice papers

Twelve practice papers and two mock examinations in each of the three papers a child actually sits. Every one is built to the item counts, mark allocation and working time set out in the Ministry’s Assessment Framework 2025–2028.

A practice paper tells you after each question. A mock tells you nothing until you submit — that is the whole point of it. Sit the mocks last, and sit them to time.

Before you write — sixty seconds

Do not skip this. More marks are lost here than anywhere else in the paper, and never because the writing was poor.

1. Which topic number are you answering?

Write it in the box on your paper too. The examiner records it, and a blank box means nobody knows which question you answered.

2. What is this topic asking you to WRITE?

3. Who is it to?

This topic makes you do these things

Tick each one as it goes in. Anything still unticked at the end is a mark you have left on the table.
