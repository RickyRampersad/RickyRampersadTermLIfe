What is inside

🎯 A plan for today, not a pile of papers

The hub looks at what you have got wrong, what you have not seen, and what is due for review, then hands you six things to work on. Answer a question wrong and that topic comes back tomorrow. Get it right repeatedly and the gap widens.

📊 Mastery by syllabus strand

Progress is tracked against the actual CSEC syllabus sections — not "Maths 62%" but "Consumer Arithmetic 45%, Algebra 81%". That is the difference between knowing you are behind and knowing what to do about it.

📚 Official sources, properly linked

Every CSEC syllabus, specimen paper and mark scheme is free from CXC, and the Ministry of Education carries worked solutions for T&T students. The hub points you at the real ones rather than a copy of unknown vintage.

🧭 Form 1 to Form 5, in order

Form 3 is when subject choices are made and Form 4 is when the syllabus starts for real. The planner shows the whole road, and only sets work from the forms you have actually reached.
