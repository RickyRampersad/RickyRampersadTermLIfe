What is inside

🎯 A plan for today, not a pile of papers

The hub looks at what you have got wrong, what you have not seen, and what is due for review, then hands you six things to work on. Answer a question wrong and that topic comes back tomorrow. Get it right repeatedly and the gap widens.

📊 Progress by strand

Progress is tracked against the strands the SEA papers test — not "Maths 62%" but "Fractions 45%, Perimeter and Area 81%". That is the difference between knowing a child is behind and knowing what to do about it.

📚 Official sources, properly linked

The Ministry of Education publishes SEA past papers, specimen papers and mark schemes free, on moe.gov.tt and its School Learning Management System. The hub points you at the real ones rather than a copy of unknown vintage.

🧭 Standard 1 to Standard 5, in order

Three papers, sat in Standard 5: Mathematics, English Language Arts and ELA Writing, weighted 100 : 60 : 40. The journey shows the whole road, and only sets work from the standards a child has actually reached.
