Resources, past papers and syllabuses

Every link here goes to the organisation that actually publishes the material — CXC, which sets the examination, and the Trinidad and Tobago Ministry of Education. All links checked reachable on 7 September 2026.

Start with the syllabus, not the past papers. CXC publishes the full CSEC syllabus for every subject free, along with specimen papers, mark schemes and subject reports. The syllabus lists precisely what an examiner may ask and how each paper is weighted — it is the single most useful document in your preparation, and almost nobody downloads it. Complete past papers are a separate, paid product sold by CXC as eBooks.

Why this hub links out instead of hosting copies

CSEC past papers are copyright CXC

CXC owns the examination papers and sells them through its own store. Scraping them and republishing them on a public website would be copying someone else's work without permission — so this hub sends you to the source instead. The syllabuses, specimen papers and mark schemes are free there anyway, and they are the more useful half.

Third-party copies are of unknown vintage

Sites offering free CSEC past-paper PDFs are common. Some are accurate; many carry papers from a superseded syllabus, and none of them tell you which. Practising a topic that was removed from the syllabus is worse than not practising at all.

The practice bank here is original

Every question in Practice was written for this hub in CSEC Paper 01 style and tagged to a syllabus strand. It is not a copy of any past paper, which is why it can sit here freely — and why it can be added to whenever a topic needs more coverage.

Use both

The hub builds the habit and finds your weak strands. Real past papers, worked under time in the months before the examination, build the stamina and the timing. Neither one replaces the other.
