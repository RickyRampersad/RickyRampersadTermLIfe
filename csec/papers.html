Resources, past papers and mark schemes

Every link here goes to the organisation that actually publishes the material: the Trinidad and Tobago Ministry of Education, which sets the Secondary Entrance Assessment. Everything on this page is free. All links were checked reachable in September 2026.

Start with the Assessment Framework, not the past papers. The Ministry publishes the document the SEA is built from, free. It names the strands in each paper, the number of items and the marks, and how the Writing paper is scored. It is the single most useful document in your child’s preparation, and almost nobody downloads it. Read it once; after that the past papers make sense.
The Ministry’s SEA papers, specimen papers and mark schemes

The Ministry gives SEA past papers away free: the real examination, not an imitation. For a child in Standard 4 or Standard 5 this is the most valuable thing on this page. The hub’s own mock papers, built to the same blueprint, are under SEA Papers.

Documents to download once, at the start of the year

Download these once and keep them in one folder: the Assessment Framework, the Standard 4 and Standard 5 curriculum guides, and the SEA information booklet. Then tick off each subject below as you find its curriculum guide on the Ministry’s primary resources page. The list is saved on this device.

Why this hub links out instead of hosting copies

The official papers are free, and they come from the Ministry

The Ministry of Education publishes SEA past papers, specimen papers and mark schemes itself, at no cost. A copy on another site adds nothing to the original and can fall behind it. This hub sends you to the source, where the paper is current and the mark scheme sits beside it.

Copies elsewhere are of unknown vintage

Sites offering SEA past-paper PDFs are common. Some are accurate; many carry papers from before the current Assessment Framework, and none of them says which. A paper built to an older framework teaches a child the wrong pace and the wrong weighting.

The practice questions here are original

Every question in Practice was written for this hub in the style of the SEA papers and tagged to a strand of the Assessment Framework. None is a copy of a past paper, which is why it can sit here freely, and why more can be added wherever a strand needs it.

Use both

The hub builds the habit and finds the weak strands. The Ministry’s real papers, worked to time in Standard 5, build the stamina and the pace: about 1 minute 50 seconds an item in Mathematics, about 2 minutes an item in English Language Arts. Neither one replaces the other.
