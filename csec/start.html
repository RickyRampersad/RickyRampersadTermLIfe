Setting up takes about three minutes

1

Make your own profile first

Open Sign in, choose Parent, and put in a name. There is no password to invent and nothing to write down. Add your e-mail if you would like a six-digit code to sign in with, and put your address on your child’s profile, so you are told every time they sign in.

2

Create a family group

In Settings → Sync across devices, create a group. You get a code like ABCD-EFGH. Enter that same code on your phone, your child's tablet and the teacher's laptop, and they all show the same progress.

3

Add your child and pick their Standard

Choose Student, enter their first name only, and tap the Standard they are in now. That one choice decides the topics, the milestones and the countdown to the SEA, so get it right. You move them up a Standard at the start of each school year, in Settings.

4

Check the subjects

Settings → Subjects starts with five of the eight primary subjects ticked: the three SEA papers, and the two that fill the rest of the week. Tick what your child's class actually does. Everything the hub sets follows from it.

5

Ten questions tonight

That is the whole routine. The hub picks what to work on. Ten a night beats three hours on a Saturday, and the streak square on your Progress page is the number to watch.

The examination, in plain terms

Mathematics

40 items, 75 marks, 75 minutes, in sections of 20, 16 and 4 items. That is about 1 minute 50 seconds an item, so pace is part of the paper.

English Language Arts

36 items, 64 marks, 75 minutes. Spelling, punctuation, grammar and reading comprehension, at about 2 minutes an item.

ELA Writing

50 minutes. Three topics, answer one. Marked on Content, Language Use, Grammar and Mechanics, and Organisation. The hub carries twelve guided methods for this paper alone.

The Secondary Entrance Assessment is set by the Ministry of Education and sat in Standard 5. The three papers are weighted 100 : 60 : 40, Mathematics first, then English Language Arts, then Writing. The SEA mark decides which secondary school a child is placed in.
What you will see

📊 Where the marks are leaking

Not "Maths 62%" but "Fractions 45%, Money 81%". You get the three topics worth asking about tonight, which is a far better conversation than "is your homework done".

🔥 Whether the habit is real

Twenty-eight squares, one per day, gold when they practised. Gaps in that grid explain most falling marks, and you will see them long before a report book does.

🎯 An honest readiness band

Built from accuracy, how much of the curriculum has been covered, whether they still remember it a week later, and the mock papers. It is a direction of travel, not a predicted SEA mark, and the hub says so above the number.

🧭 What this year is actually for

Five years lead to one examination, sat in Standard 5. The Journey page lays out each Standard term by term, so the mock papers and the writing practice arrive when they should and nothing comes as a surprise.

Three honest limits, so nothing surprises you later.

1. This does not replace school, a teacher, or the real past papers. It builds the nightly habit and finds the weak topics. The Ministry publishes past SEA papers, specimen papers and mark schemes free of charge; the Resources page links them, and in Standard 5 your child should sit them to time.

2. The readiness band is our own indicator from practice inside the hub. Nobody has checked it against a real SEA result yet, because there has not been one. Treat school reports as the truth.

Setting up a class

1

Create a teacher profile and a class group

Sign in as Teacher, then in Settings → Sync across devices create a group of type Class. Give the code to the pupils who want to join, or enter it on their devices yourself.

2

Pupils join with the code

Each pupil creates their own profile on their own device and enters the class code in Settings. Their practice then appears on your roster. Ask them to use a first name or initials. There is no need for full names.

3

Read the class weak topics

Your Class page ranks the topics the whole group is weakest on. Anything with several pupils behind it is a lesson, not a worksheet.

4

Set one focus topic at a time

A focus topic appears at the top of that pupil's desk with your note attached, until they clear it. One thing, not five.

What the teacher view gives you

Roster with real numbers

Questions answered, accuracy, streak, last active and weakest topic for every pupil who has joined the group, refreshed whenever they sync.

Class-wide weak topics

Combined accuracy per topic across the whole class, so you can see what to reteach rather than guessing from marked books.

Five-year record

Term-by-term accuracy and curriculum coverage from Standard 1 onwards, with each move up a Standard marked. Useful at parents' evening and when the Standard 5 year begins.

Mock papers to the Ministry's blueprint

Each of the three papers, sat to time, no feedback until submission, a full review afterwards. The results feed the readiness band, weighted more heavily than untimed practice.

A note on data. Only what pupils type into the hub is stored: a display name, Standard, school, subject list and practice performance. No date of birth, no contact details, no school marks. A pupil or parent can remove themselves from a group at any time in Settings, and that deletes their record from the shared group immediately. Please do not put anything into a name field that you would not put on a noticeboard.
Does it cost anything?+

No. It is free for branch families and there is nothing to install. It runs in any browser on a phone, tablet or laptop. There is no advertising and nothing is sold.

What is the password?+

There isn’t one. Choose your name and you are in. A child should not be locked out of their own homework by something they cannot remember.

If you would rather have a check, put an e-mail address on the profile and the hub will send a six-digit code instead. On a child’s profile use a parent’s address: the code lands with the parent, so a parent hears about every sign-in without having to ask. The code lasts ten minutes and asking for a new one cancels the old.

Codes need the sync service to be deployed. Until it is, signing in is by name only, and Settings → Who signed in still records every one.

Can I see who has been signing in?+

Yes. Settings → Who signed in shows who, when, and which door. Refused attempts are listed too. It is kept on the device, so it works with no internet; with sync set up, one list covers every phone and laptop in the group.

My child got a new phone. Do we lose everything?+

Not if you are in a sync group. Install nothing. Open the hub on the new phone, create a profile, and enter the group code in Settings. Everything comes down. If you are not using sync, use Settings → Export backup on the old device and restore the file on the new one.

Is my child's data safe?+

Be clear-eyed about this. If you use a sync group, the names and practice scores in that group are stored on a Google Sheet owned by the branch, and anyone holding the group code can read and change them. The code is the only lock. There is no password. Share it with family and your child's teacher and nobody else, and if it ever gets out, delete the group in Settings and make a new one. If you would rather nothing left the device at all, simply do not create a group: the hub works fully without one.

Where are the past papers?+

The Ministry of Education publishes past SEA papers, specimen papers and mark schemes free of charge, on moe.gov.tt and its School Learning Management System. We do not copy them; we link them. The Resources page lists 25 of them. The practice questions and mock papers in the hub are our own, written to the Ministry's blueprint, so the shape of each paper is familiar before the real one is opened.

What is on the writing paper?+

Fifty minutes, three topics, and the child answers one. It is marked on four things: Content, Language Use, Grammar and Mechanics, and Organisation. The hub's guided methods for the writing paper take each of those in turn, and the mock papers put a writing paper in front of the child under time.

Does the readiness band predict an SEA mark?+

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. It is built from practice inside this hub, which flatters: multiple choice, no time pressure, an answer after every question. It weights retention and the mock papers precisely because of that, and it shows nothing at all until there is enough evidence. Treat it as a direction of travel. School reports and the real past papers are the truth.

My child does different subjects.+

Go to Settings → Subjects. Eight are available, five are ticked by default. Set what the class actually does and the daily plan, the mastery rings and the topic browser all follow it.

How much should they actually do?+

Ten questions a night, every night. That is it. A twenty-five minute timer is built into the desk. In Standard 5 add one mock paper a week and the Ministry's past papers, sat to time. Consistency beats intensity by a distance, which is why the streak is on the front page.

Can a teacher see my child without me knowing?+

Only if they have your group code. Nothing is shared by default and there is no directory. A group is invisible to anyone without its code. You can remove your child from a group at any time in Settings, which deletes their record from it immediately.

Something is wrong or missing. Who do I tell?+

Tell the branch. The question bank, the guided methods and the subject list all grow, and a specific report, such as "the Standard 4 Fractions questions run out", is exactly what makes the next version better.
