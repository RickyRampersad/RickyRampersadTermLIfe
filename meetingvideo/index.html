Signing in to the branch meeting

A minute and a quarter: how to get in, and what we expect of you in the room.

There are two taps, not one.
Your agent number gets you into the room. The “I am here” button on the meeting card is the register — it time-stamps you. Sign in and never press it, and the register has you absent.
Step oneOpen rickyrampersadbranch.com/meetings on any phone.
Step twoType your agent number — the one on your card. No password.
Step threePress “I am here”. That tap is the register.
Looking in?Sign in as 0000 and give your name and your role. Visitors are never counted on the branch register.
