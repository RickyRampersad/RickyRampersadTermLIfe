What you're covered for

VUMI® Travel VIP benefits

Travel VIP is a genuine international medical plan, not a basic travel policy. You choose the doctor and the hospital, anywhere in the world, and VUMI pays.

🩺 Pre-existing medical conditions
Travel VIP is unusual among travel plans in that it does cover pre-existing conditions — up to US$500,000. To qualify, the condition must have been stable for at least 6 months before you travel: no symptoms, no new treatment and no change in medication or dosage.

If a condition was diagnosed, treated or had medication adjusted inside that 6-month window, it will not be covered. If you're unsure, declare it on the application and let us check it with VUMI before you pay — that's free, and far better than finding out at a hospital.
🚁 Evacuation & repatriation
If you're somewhere that can't treat you properly, VUMI arranges and pays for emergency medical evacuation to the nearest facility that can — including air ambulance where medically necessary.

If the worst happens, repatriation of mortal remains is covered so your family isn't left arranging and funding it from Trinidad. Travel VIP also covers the airfare for a family member to fly out and be with you if you're hospitalised abroad, and a return flight home if illness ends your trip early.
🕐 The VIP service — what it actually means
· 24/7 emergency assistance — one number, answered any hour, in English or Spanish.
· Free choice of provider — no network restrictions. Walk into the hospital you want.
· Direct billing — VUMI settles with the hospital where possible, so you're not fronting thousands on a credit card.
· Second medical opinion — included on annual plans, from an international specialist panel.
· Children under 2 free — infants travel at no premium.
🚫 What isn't covered
Common exclusions on travel medical plans include: conditions that weren't stable for the required period; travel undertaken against medical advice or specifically to obtain treatment; pregnancy and childbirth beyond defined limits; self-inflicted injury; injury while under the influence of alcohol or drugs; professional or high-risk sports; war and civil unrest; and claims arising in a country you were advised not to travel to.

This is a summary, not the full list. Ask us for the current Travel VIP policy wording before you buy and we'll send it across — you should read the exclusions section properly.
Questions

Before you buy

The things people ask us most about travel cover.

Single trip or annual — which is cheaper for me?
Rule of thumb: if you'll take two or more international trips in the next 12 months, the annual plan almost always works out cheaper than buying single-trip cover each time — and you never have to think about it again. One trip a year, and single-trip wins. Our quote engine checks both for you automatically and tells you which is better.
Do I really need this if my credit card includes travel cover?
Card cover is usually capped far lower than you'd think — often US$50,000 or less — and typically requires that you booked the whole trip on that card. A single night in a US intensive care unit can run past US$20,000. Read your card's limit; if it's under US$500,000, it will not cover a serious event abroad.
What if my trip runs longer than planned?
Single-trip cover can be extended as many times as you need, as long as the extensions are consecutive and the total doesn't exceed 365 days. On annual plans, if one trip runs past your 30, 45 or 90-day limit, it can be extended for an additional daily premium. Contact us before the original cover expires — extending is straightforward, restarting after a gap is not.
Is there an age limit?
Cover is available up to age 79. Travellers aged 70–79 are covered to a US$500,000 maximum rather than US$5,000,000. Children under 2 are covered free of premium. If you're 80 or over, we have other options — contact Ricky directly and we'll find something.
How quickly can I be covered?
Usually the same working day. Once you submit this application, we key it into the VUMI portal, take the premium, and VUMI issues your certificate by email. If you're travelling within 48 hours, say so on the form and message Ricky — we'll prioritise it.
