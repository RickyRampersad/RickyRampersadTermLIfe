What each benefit covers, in Guardian General's words
Personal accident (age limit 5 to 75)Accidental bodily injury resulting in:
  1. death, loss of one or more limbs, all sight in one or both eyes, the power of speech or the sense of hearing, or permanent total disablement
  2. temporary total disablement
  3. temporary partial disablement
Baggage, personal effects and travel documentsYour passport, driver's licence, valid forms of identification, trunks, suitcases and similar containers, their contents, what you wear and carry-on luggage (excluding furs, household goods, furniture and the like) against loss or damage by accident or misfortune in transit and at your destination. Up to 10% of the sum insured for immediate necessities bought at your outward destination after 24 hours' loss or misdirection of baggage by the carriers. Any one unspecified item is limited to $1,000; a pair or set of articles is one item.
Additional expenses due to loss of passport or driver's licenceReasonable additional travel or accommodation expenses you have to incur to replace a lost or stolen passport or driver's licence while abroad.
Medical expenses (age limit 5 to 75)Expenses necessarily incurred as a result of accidental bodily injury, sickness or disease:
  1. medical, surgical, hospital or nursing-home charges, prescribed drugs, emergency dental or optical charges
  2. additional accommodation, travel or other expenses, including those of another person required to stay or travel with you
  3. the cost of conveying your body or ashes to your home address
Loss of money and ticketsLoss of travel tickets and money, including cash withdrawn from an ATM by a legitimate card, as a result of assault or robbery on the trip within two hours of the withdrawal.
Loss of deposits, curtailment and missed connections (age limit 5 to 75)Loss of deposits paid, or payment for unused accommodation or travel, on cancellation or postponement of the trip due to: (a) your death, bodily injury, sickness or disease; (b) that of any relative or travel companion you have arranged to travel with; (c) you or your relative or travel companion being called for witness or jury service; (d) missing your connecting flight through the carrier's delay from inclement weather or common airline causes, where the delay exceeds five hours.
