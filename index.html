Questions

The things people ask us first

Straight answers, including the ones where the answer is “no”.

What does life insurance actually cost me?
It depends on your age, your health and how much cover you want — which is why every product on this page opens its own quotation rather than showing one price. Xpress Life is the fastest way to see a real number: five health questions, no medicals, and a quote in one sitting. If you would rather work out how much cover you need before you price it, start with the FactFind360 needs analysis.
Do I have to take a medical exam?
Not for Xpress Life — it is a simplified instant-issue product for qualifying applicants, and five health questions replace the medical. Larger sums assured and some other products are underwritten normally, and we tell you up front if a medical is needed before you commit to anything.
Do I need to come into the office?
No. Quotes, applications, claims, renewals and service reviews all run online from your phone, and they reach the branch team directly. You are welcome to come in — we are at 9–13 Endeavour 1st Street, Chaguanas, Monday to Friday — but nothing on this site requires it.
My agent retired or stopped calling. What do I do?
You have what the industry calls an orphan policy, and it is more common than most people think. We trace the policy, fix your records and tell you honestly what we can and cannot do — free, with no obligation to buy anything, in about four minutes. That is the whole purpose of donthaveanagent.com.
Can you service a policy from another insurance company?
Not usually, and we would rather say so than waste your time. We service policies issued by Guardian Life of the Caribbean. If your policy is with another insurer we will tell you plainly and point you to the right place — and we will still answer your question while you are here.
How do I file a claim?
Through Claims TT. Photograph your documents, upload them from your phone, and you get a claim reference at the end — usually about ten minutes. You can track the claim afterwards and see exactly which documents we are still waiting on. If you would rather do it by phone, call the branch on (868) 678-5921.
Is my information safe on these forms?
Yes. Every form on this site is served over HTTPS and submits straight to the branch team — client records live in our secured systems, never on this website. We ask only for what the product or the claim actually needs.
I want to become an advisor. How does that work?
There are five gates, deliberately: apply and screen, meet the Branch Manager, discovery sessions, a scientific assessment, then final selection. Applications are reviewed within 48 hours, newcomers are fully trained before they are contracted, and conversations with practising advisors are confidential. Start at the Careers section above.
