Questions

The things people ask us first

What does life insurance actually cost me?

Less than most people expect, and it depends on your age, your health and how much cover you want. Rather than guess, open a quote — it's free, takes a few minutes and commits you to nothing. The FactFind360 needs analysis will also show you how much cover actually fits your situation.

Do I have to take a medical exam?

Not always. Xpress Life is instant-issue for qualifying applicants — five health questions and no medicals. Larger sums assured and some health histories do need underwriting, and we'll tell you up front which applies to you.

Do I need to come into the office?

No. Every quote, application, claim and service request on this page can be completed from your phone. You're welcome to walk in to Endeavour 1st Street if you'd rather do it in person — but nothing here requires it.

My agent retired or stopped calling. What do I do?

That's an orphan policy, and it's common. donthaveanagent.com is built for exactly this: we trace the policy, correct your records and tell you honestly what we can and cannot do. Free, no obligation, and you don't need your policy number.

Can you service a policy from another insurance company?

We can review it and tell you where you stand. Servicing itself stays with the insurer that issued it — if your policy wasn't issued by Guardian Life of the Caribbean we'll point you to the right company rather than leave you guessing.

How do I file a claim?

Through Claims TT. Photograph your documents, upload them from your phone, and you'll get a claim reference in about ten minutes. You can track the claim afterwards and see exactly what we're still waiting on.

Is my information safe on these forms?

Yes. Every form here is served over HTTPS and goes directly to our branch team. We use your information to look after your policy — it is never sold, and it is never disclosed without your express consent.

I want to become an advisor. How does that work?

Five gates, set out in the Careers section above. Newcomers get full training, induction and boot camp before they're contracted. Practising advisors: conversations are fully confidential, and we review applications within 48 hours.
