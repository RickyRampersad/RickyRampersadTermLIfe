Reporting your day

Four blocks. Four short reports. Under five minutes across a whole day — and it is the difference between saying you did the work and showing it.

Why this exists

It is not extra admin. It is already in your job document.

Your Sales Support Assistant job document lists six responsibilities: New Application Process · Pending, Lapse & Follow-ups · Document Management · Reporting · Administrative Support · and other duties as assigned.

Your four KPI blocks are those same responsibilities, in the order your day runs. Reporting is number four on that list — reporting on your work is part of the job, not something added on top of it.

From your job document
"All completed applications must be submitted to Head Office within 2 days of receipt from agent."
"All policy related documents to be submitted to the relevant party within one (1) day of receipt."
"All policy related documents to be prepared or processed with 100% accuracy at all times."
Those are the standards you are measured against. A block report is how anyone — including you — knows whether they were met.

Your day

Already set up. You do not build it each morning.

Open /kpi/ and sign in with your email or agent number. You land on your own day, and only your own — nobody else's record is visible to you.

Your hours, your lunch and your four blocks are already there, each showing what it is for. Sasha's 8–10 says Premium Dues / Surveys. That comes from the branch schedule, so it no longer has to be emailed out and read off a table every morning.

The four squares The strip at the top of your day shows one square per block. Grey means not yet reported, green means in. Four green is a finished day.

Reporting a block

At the end of the block — not at four o'clock from memory.

1

Open the block you just finished

Tap its row. The KPI worked is already chosen from your schedule, so you are confirming it rather than starting from a blank page. If the block went somewhere else, change it — that is what the list is for.

2

Three lines that are not the same line

FieldWhat belongs there
ActionedWhat you worked on. Names, counts, cases — "18 premium dues sent out", not "worked on premium dues".
Resolved / closedWhat is genuinely finished. If it is still moving, it is not resolved.
Still open & owned by youWhat you carry into tomorrow. Being honest here is not a bad mark — it is how work gets help.
3

If you are waiting on somebody

Name them, and say when you last chased. "Waiting on Head Office" is not a blocker; "Kerwyn — chased Tuesday and again today" is.

The branch rule A rolled due date is not an actioned task. Moving a date without a genuine blocker and a named owner reads as work done when nothing moved.
4

Submit the block

Press Submit block. That block is written and stamped with the time; the other three are untouched. You can submit again later to correct it — it updates rather than adding a second entry.

You get your own copy The moment you submit, your own words arrive in your inbox: what you wrote, how many blocks are in, and which are still to come. That is the copy to post to the branch group — no retyping.

If the block included training

Choose Training delivered as the KPI and a training record opens: who you trained, the topic, objectives set against met, whether you tested them and the result, and the follow-up. It is kept as its own line in the branch's training register, so the branch has a record of who was brought up to speed on what.

The day's own fields

These belong to the day, not to any one block. Save them once — Save the day.

FieldWhat it is for
Task positionClosed today, overdue now, and how many of those are 60+ days. Straight from Salesforce.
Value AddedSomething the branch would not have unless you brought it. If there genuinely was nothing, write "None today" — the honest entry counts.
Innovation / ideaA tool, tracker, checklist, macro or process fix you spotted. The friction, and your fix.
System flagsWhat broke, and who you flagged it to.
Why the last two are not optional decoration Your job document, under Continuous Improvement: "demonstrate by making recommendations." Under Curiosity: "be proactive in sharing views or different perspectives to aid with improving service offerings." Those two boxes are where you get the credit for exactly that.

What happens after you submit

Who sees what, and when.

On submit You get your block back by email — your copy for the branch group.
12:00 If your morning is still blank, you get a reminder naming which blocks are outstanding.
15:00 If blocks 1–3 are not all in, you get asked for them, and for your final block.
15:00 The Branch Manager gets the branch at the cut — who logged, who did not, every blocker raised.
Friday 17:00 The week, against the week before — closed, overdue, aged, value added, ideas, training.
If you are up to date you hear nothing The reminders look at what has actually been submitted and skip anyone who is current. They only arrive when something is genuinely missing.

What it becomes

This is the part worth caring about.

Every block you report builds a record — closed against overdue, days logged, value added, ideas raised, training delivered. Your Trends view is that record, and it is in your own words with the dates attached.

When your appraisal comes round, that is the evidence. Not a memory of a good month — a hundred small entries you made at the time.

A week reported properly Reports in 25/25 · closed 236 → 463 · overdue 146 → 68 · 60+ days 44 → 16 · value added 0 → 10. Nobody was argued into that. The blocks were reported as they were worked, so the branch could see what was actually happening while there was still time to act on it.

The standard

  • Completed applications to Head Office within 2 days of receipt.
  • Policy documents processed with 100% accuracy.
  • Documents to the relevant party within 1 day of receipt.
  • A rolled due date is not an actioned task — log the genuine blocker and the owner.
  • "Waiting on someone" needs a named person and the date last chased.
  • Value Added is logged every day — even when the honest entry is "None today".
  • Report each block as it ends, not at the end of the day.

If something goes wrong

"Session expired. Sign in again." Sign-ins last 12 hours. Expected each morning.
You submitted the wrong thing Open the block again, correct it, and submit. It updates the same entry — it does not add a second one.
You forgot yesterday Change the Reporting day at the top and submit against that date. Late is better than missing, and the time stamp is honest about when it was written.
You cannot sign in Five wrong tries locks the account for fifteen minutes. Wait it out, or ask the Branch Manager — your log-in is on the Access tab of the branch workbook.
