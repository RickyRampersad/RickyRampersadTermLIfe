The three rules, for everyone

To read
  1. In by 12 noon, down at 1 pm. Papers in the branch's hands before 12 noon on a working day go on that day's 1 pm bag. Anything later goes on the next working day's bag. Friday after 12 is Monday's bag.
  2. Logged is not handed in. Logging tells the branch what is coming. Nothing goes down until a staff member has the papers in hand and signs for them.
  3. One sign-in. Your agent number and the password you use for the fact find. Staff can also use their RR Operations access code. What you see depends on your role.
Why: in our old book, 57% of documents reached us after 12 noon, and only 41% went down the day they came in. These three rules are how we change that.
1

Sign in

To read

Open rickyrampersadbranch.com/logbook. Enter your agent number and your fact find password. Your phone stays signed in until you press Sign out.

The bag bar at the top shows the time left to 12 noon, who has the bag this week, and whether papers handed in now catch today's bag.

2

Log a document

To read
  1. Choose the document first. The Log Book suggests the department it has gone to before. You can change it; the line records the change.
  2. Find the client by name, e-mail or policy number, then tick their policies. You see only clients on your own policies.
  3. Not in Salesforce yet? Type the client in. The line is flagged for staff to check.
  4. Log it. You get a reference at once, and the screen tells you whether the papers catch today's bag if they are in by 12 noon.
3

Nothing left behind

To read

The Log Book learned from three years of our own book which documents travel together: an ACH form and a bank statement, a surrender and proof of ID. When one usually comes with another and you have not added it, it asks. Add it with one tap, or log anyway and the line is flagged.

4

Hand it in

To read

Bring the papers to the branch. Staff tick them on the Queue and sign for them, and from then on they are the branch's.

  • 11:00: if you have papers logged and not handed in, you get an e-mail: one hour to catch today's bag.
  • Still not in after a working day you are reminded again on day 3. Papers never handed in count against you in the Log Book's quality score.
5

My log

To read

Every line you logged, with each step stamped: Logged, Received, Sent on the transmittal, and the department's answer three working days later.

  • Withdraw works only while a line still reads Logged, before staff receive it.
  • Returned: staff send papers back with a reason by e-mail when something is missing or unsigned. Put them right and log them again.
6

Contracts you deliver

To read

Log a policy contract you collect as Contract for delivery. The Act (section 155(6)) gives 20 days to put it in the client's hands.

  • You are reminded on day 15 and day 18. The branch manager is told on day 20.
  • Record the delivery in the Delivery Book: that closes the line by itself.
7

Unit managers

To read

Sign in the same way. You log your own clients like an agent, and My team shows your own papers, the whole team's, or one agent's. You can withdraw only your own lines.

1

Sign in and your screens

To read

Open rickyrampersadbranch.com/logbook and sign in with your agent number and fact find password, or your access code. You get the staff side: the Queue, Transmittals and Insights.

2

The bag day and whose bag it is

To read
  • Until 12 noon: papers come in and are received.
  • 12 noon: the bag closes; they are checked and put on transmittals.
  • 1 pm: the bag comes in, is cleared, the mail given out, and it goes back down with the day's transmittals.
The rota: Azariah, then Liz, then Sasha, one week each, Azariah from Monday 19 October. Kamla does not do transmittals. Sasha leaves at 2, Liz at 3 and Azariah at 4, so the bag is done by 1 pm while all three are in.
3

Receive and sign

To read
  1. On the Queue, tick the lines whose papers are in your hand.
  2. Check each department. Correct it if it is wrong: the Log Book learns from your correction.
  3. Press Receive & sign and sign. The date collected is now stamped.

Something missing or unsigned? Return to the agent with the reason. The agent gets an e-mail and logs it again once it is right.

4

12 noon: the transmittals

To read

At 12:00 whoever has the bag gets an e-mail of what to prepare, by department.

A transmittal is for one department. Choose its lines and press Build transmittal: it prints with the date collected, the date sent and any reason. OFT (FedEx) needs the tracking number.

5

1 pm: the bag, and anything late

To read
  • Press Bag in when the bag is cleared and the mail given out, and Bag out when it is sealed and gone.
  • A line still here after its bag has gone is late. A transmittal will not take it without a reason, and the reason prints beside the dates.
  • From 1 pm the Log Book asks for that reason by e-mail, to whoever received the papers and whoever has the bag, sales support and the agent copied.
6

Three working days on

To read

The department is asked by e-mail whether the transmittal arrived: Yes or No. Yes is recorded. No asks what is missing, goes to sales support for a response, and opens a High-priority Salesforce task, due the next working day, for whoever collected the papers.

7

Contracts, Insights and the 5 pm digest

To read
  • Contracts with agents on the Queue shows each contract's day out of 20. A delivery in the Delivery Book, or Delivered, closes it.
  • Insights: the share that made its bag, by hour received, by department and by agent.
  • 5 pm: the branch manager gets the day's digest, sales support copied: late lines with no reason, departments that said No or have not answered, contracts at day 15 or more, papers logged and not handed in.
