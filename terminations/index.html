When someone leaves your plan

From your own group page: Guardian Life’s policy change form filled from our records, sent to EB Customer Care with our branch copied, and followed up until it’s done.

    1. 1

      Find them

      Sign in to your group page, choose Guardian’s forms, then change or terminate, and search by name or certificate number.

    2. 2

      Give the date

      Everything we hold fills the form. You give the date cover ends; anything we don’t have is listed, never guessed.

    3. 3

      We follow it through

      It goes to EB Customer Care with our branch copied. Your account manager follows it up every three working days until it’s done.
