One portal, three states — not two products

Quotes and billing are not different systems. They are the same company, before and after they sign. Here is what that means, and what it looks like.

The decision

Both options work. Only one of them stops you answering a question every month.

Recommended

One portal, rooms that appear

Billing is not a separate product — it is a section that does not exist until a company is on the books. The seam is prospect versus client, not quotes versus billing.

  • Same data. The census that prices the plan is the census that bills it. The cover levels chosen at quote are the sums assured on the billing.
  • The reconciliation only works here. Life read against health needs the whole group in one place.
  • One sign-in. You already have two employer doors and it already hurts.
  • Every billing visit walks past your quoting tools.
The alternative

A separate billing portal

Defensible, and it is what most agencies end up with — usually by accident rather than choice.

  • The group record, the census and the member list all exist twice, and drift.
  • A third employer sign-in on top of the two you have.
  • “Which link do I send them?” changes the day they sign.
  • The monthly habit lives in the plain app; the sales story lives in the polished one.

The wall
An employee is the fourth state — their own sign-in, one room: what they are covered for, who their beneficiaries are, and what happens if they leave. Same portal, smallest door.

The order I would do it in

  1. Fold the billing pages into the portal. One navigation, existing codes still working so nothing breaks while the sign-in question is still open. Half a day.
  2. Pick one authentication scheme. Query Pal's access codes, or email and password on the branch script. Not both. This is a decision, not a build.
  3. Write the benefitswall endpoint so the wall and the billing pages read the real book instead of a demonstration one.
  4. Then, and only then, put real member data behind it.
The cost of merging, stated plainly. The day live billing sits in that portal, the whole site inherits the security bar of the most sensitive thing in it. The employer tab currently admits anyone with any email. Merging does not create that problem — it removes your ability to postpone it.
