Your plan

Guardian keeps group life and group health on two policies and asks for changes on two different forms. You fill this once and both come out filled — which is also how life and health stop disagreeing about who is covered.

Both numbers are printed at the top of your monthly billing. If you only run one of the two plans, leave the other blank and that form is not produced.

Who changed this month

Nothing added yet. Every person you add here appears on whichever Guardian form applies to them — you never fill the same name twice.

Sign it

Guardian will not action a movement without an authorised signature, so this is the part that makes it real rather than a note.

You sign the printed forms by hand and stamp them; that is what Guardian's department requires. Sending this to us first means we check it before it goes anywhere.
