05 What runs on its own

The daily sweep, 3PM

One trigger drives the whole autopilot. Every afternoon it walks every row and, case by case, decides what is owed:

  • past deadline and still open → chase the department on the thread;
  • open and quiet for a few days → update the client;
  • on hold → tell the client once what that means, then stay quiet;
  • just closed → send the rating email;
  • a termination → conversion reminders on their own schedule;
  • unassigned → assign it from the KPI table.

Separately, a reply watcher reads department replies as they arrive: it stamps the reply time, pauses the chasers, and closes the case when the reply plainly says it is done.
