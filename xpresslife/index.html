Straight answers

How fast do I actually get covered?
Your rate shows in seconds and the application takes about 5 minutes. It then goes to Guardian Life's underwriters, who review it and decide; the branch cannot approve a policy. Our team confirms your details with you and keeps you updated at every step. No medical exam for most applicants.
What does my family get if something happens early?
Year 1: the premiums paid are returned. Year 2: half your cover is paid. Year 3 onward: your full cover is paid. If you are 18 to 59, the optional Accidental Death Benefit pays from the first day when death is caused by an accident.
When do my payments stop?
After 20 years. Your premium is fixed from day one and never increases. After year 20 you never pay again, and your cover carries on to about age 100.
What is the year-21 money-back option?
In year 21 only, you may give up the policy and receive every premium you paid, without interest; the policy then ends. Guardian writes to you before year 21 to remind you. There is no cash value in any other year. Or keep the policy, and if it is in force at about age 100 the full cover is paid to you in cash.
What's not covered?
We show you the fine print upfront. Suicide within the first two years pays a refund of premiums only. Death caused directly or indirectly by war or insurrection is excluded. And untrue answers on the application can void the policy entirely — which is exactly why we ask you to answer honestly.
Who can apply?
Ages 18 to 69 who can honestly answer No to all five health questions on Guardian's declaration. That makes it a plan for people in good health, not for everyone. If you're 70 or over, or a question is a Yes for you, talk to our team about other Guardian plans.
What if I miss a payment?
A 30-day grace period applies. After three years of premiums, Guardian must give you written notice before the policy can lapse. A lapsed policy can be reinstated once, within three years, with evidence of good health and all missed premiums paid, and the year-1 and year-2 reduced benefits start again from the reinstatement.
