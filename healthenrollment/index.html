Joining your group health plan

One link from your HR team, a few minutes on the employee’s phone, signed on the screen, and Guardian Life’s own forms filled in.

    1. 1

      Your HR team sends one link

      Your company and the date cover starts travel with it, on WhatsApp or by e-mail.

    2. 2

      The employee does their part

      Their details, their family, who the life cover pays, one tick for the health questions, and a signature on the screen.

    3. 3

      We do the rest

      Guardian’s own application and declaration, filled in. We check every page, send it to Guardian and follow it through.
