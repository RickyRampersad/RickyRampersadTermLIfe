Joining your group plan, start to finish

One link from your HR team, the employee’s own part on their phone, your check and approval, then Guardian Life’s own form straight to Guardian, with every step on the record.

    1. 1

      Your HR team sends their link

      From your service page: their name, their e-mail, the plans and the start date. Their own link reaches them by e-mail and stays open sixty days.

    2. 2

      The employee does their part

      Their details, their family, who the life cover pays, the health questions and a signature on the screen. A reminder each working day if they have not finished.

    3. 3

      You check and approve

      Guardian’s own form, already filled in, and the employer’s part. You never see their health answers.

    4. 4

      Guardian Life, and we follow it through

      Sent to Guardian’s group benefits team with our branch copied, and followed up every three working days until the cover is in place.
