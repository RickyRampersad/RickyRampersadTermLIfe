The process

Seven steps, and you see every one

This is not how most of the industry does it. It is how this branch does it, on every single file, whether you buy anything or not.

  1. The hour itself
    You and your advisor

    You sit down together

    About an hour. Bring nothing. What you earn, what you owe, who depends on you — and what you want for them. No products are mentioned yet.

  2. Same hour
    In front of you

    The numbers are worked out live

    Not later, not from memory in the car. It is typed while you talk and the figures move as you answer. You watch your own arithmetic happen.

  3. Same hour
    Your advisor

    Every recommendation gets its reason

    Not just what to buy — why that one, for you, in plain words, written down beside it. If a reason cannot be written, the recommendation does not go forward.

  4. Within 24 hours
    Independent of your advisor

    A manager checks the work — before you see it

    Somebody who is not selling you anything reads the whole file, tests the recommendation against your numbers, and signs it. If it does not hold up, it goes back to your advisor. You never see it until it has passed.

  5. Day 2
    Sent to you

    You get your own copy

    In plain language, to your own inbox, before anyone asks you to sign anything. Your needs, what was recommended, and the reason for each one.

  6. Your own time
    Your decision

    You take all of it, some of it, or none of it

    You can agree, take part of it, or say no — and your reason is recorded just as carefully as the recommendation was. Saying no is a legitimate answer here, and it is written down as one.

  7. Only then
    Only then

    You sign, and it is submitted

    The paperwork comes last, not first. And the file keeps the whole record — what you were told, why, who checked it, and what you decided.
