Step 2 of 5

Employee — Declaration 1

Everything about the employee: their details, contribution, plan, beneficiary — and the Declaration 1 form.

Why this section: Declaration 1 is the employee’s part of the application. Their personal details support underwriting and KYC; their contribution is their own tax deduction; the beneficiary receives the benefit.

AEmployee details

Why we ask: the address must match the utility bill (KYC); occupation and salary must match the payslip the BIR sees; marital status guides the beneficiary advice.

How the employee completes it

  1. Proposer block auto-fills (name, surname, DOB).
  2. A and B come pre-circled on Part 1.E, and the date is prefilled.
  3. The employee just signs Part 1.E, then signs page 5 item 7.

Step 3 of 5

Declaration 2 — Third Party

The company’s declaration (it owns the policy).

Why this form: because the company — not the employee — owns a TopHat policy, the law requires a third-party declaration where the company commits as Proposer.

How it's completed (company-owned)

  1. Company name → Proposer First-Name + Legal Entity; incorporation date in the date field.
  2. Employee → Annuitant / Life-Assured block.
  3. A and B come pre-circled on Part 2.D.
  4. Company signs & stamps on top; employee signs underneath — on 2.D and page 5 item 7.

Step 4 of 5

KYC Documents

Two documents — tick them off as collected.

Why: the law requires identity and address verification before any policy can be issued. Missing KYC is the #1 cause of delays.
Photo ID — driver's permit or national ID card.
Proof of address — utility bill in the client's name (under 6 months old).
Recent pay slip — must match the salary entered (BIR requires the original or a certified copy).
