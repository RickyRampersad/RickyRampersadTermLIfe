What the Act asks of us.

Every one of these was already an obligation. What changed is that something now answers it, the same way, every time, without anyone having to remember.

“The consumer certifies the accuracy of the information recorded.”
→
The client’s YesThey read it, confirm it and sign before anyone approves.
Guideline 5.2.2(b)–(c)
“Plain language, and the advice given in writing.”
→
The letterBranch letterhead, the Act quoted in full, your name at the top.
Schedule 11 E · Guideline 5.4.3
“Post-sale communication, updates and periodic reviews.”
→

“An agent shall not cause a policyholder to replace a long-term policy without first discussing the advantages and disadvantages.”

Who does what now

Four cards, one client.

The system sends every client email and chases every stall. These are the few things each of us still does by hand.

🤵
Card 1The advisor
You do
  • Tap Submit with the client’s email on the fact find. The draft goes in your name.
  • Day 8, no answer: call the client when the email asks you to.
  • Four taps, as they happen — the moment it happens, not at the end of the week.
  • Deliver inside 20 days of the policy reaching the branch.
  • Book the yearly review when the client’s answer lands in your inbox.
The system does
  • The draft, the reminders, the milestones, the survey, the yearly review. All in your name, replies to you.
Never
  • Ask a client to print or wet-sign the fact find.
🔍
Card 2The direct manager
You do
  • Decide within one business day of the client’s Yes.
  • Read the copies. You are copied on every email your advisors’ clients get.
  • Act on the chases: nothing keyed at 7 days, undelivered at 10.
  • Anything you send back, follow. It is swept daily now, but the correction is still a conversation.
The system does
  • Holds Agree until the client has confirmed and signed. Tells you the moment they do.
Never
  • Approve before the client’s Yes. Only the branch manager’s link opens that door, and it is recorded.
🗃
Card 3Sales support
You do
  • Key the application the day it comes in, and tell the advisor to tap Signed and submitted.
  • When a policy arrives, tell the advisor the same day — their tap starts the delivery clock.
  • Fill in the dates. Dispatch, receipt, delivery. A blank date is a client who never hears from us.
The system does
  • Everything the client hears. Nothing goes from Sales Support’s address.
Never
  • Email a client about their plan. You are not copied, by design.
🛡️
Card 4The branch manager
You do
  • Read every low score personally — advice ratings and delivery surveys of 3 or under. They come to you alone.
  • Escalations: a policy 15 days at the branch undelivered lands with you.
  • The override, rarely: approve before a client’s Yes, from your own link, with a reason.
The system does
  • Blind-copies you on every client email. Stamps every send. Never sends twice.
Never
  • Show a low rating against an advisor’s name on the wall.
Thank you. Genuinely.

None of this came from a system. It came from advisors saying the same thing often enough that it became impossible to ignore. Keep telling us what is not working — that is how the next nine get built.
