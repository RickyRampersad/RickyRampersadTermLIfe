The short version

Nobody owned the silence.

A client signed, and then heard nothing until their policy arrived — sometimes weeks. Nobody forgot. It was simply nobody’s job to tell them. Eight things changed. None of them adds a form to your day.

01

The client’s Yes comes first

After you tap Submit, your client gets the draft in your name. They read it, confirm it is right, rate the advice, choose how much they want to hear from us, and sign online. No printing. No wet signature on the fact find.

Silent client? Reminded on day 3 and day 8 — and on day 8 you are asked to call.

02

No manager can approve until it is in

Not by email, not on the dashboard, not on the review page. All three doors are closed the same way.

Only the branch manager can approve past the wait, with a reason, and it is written on the case.

03

Every client email is yours

Sent in your name, replies come back to you. Your direct manager is copied, the branch manager is blind-copied, and Sales Support is never copied — by design.

04

No email on the fact find?

You get a link the same minute to add one. Nothing can reach a client without an address, so the case simply waits for you rather than failing quietly.

05

Two days after delivery

Five questions on how it went, answered on a phone in under a minute. Three or under goes to the branch manager alone — never to the wall, never against your name.

06

Every year, before the birthday

Thirty days ahead: has anything changed? The answer lands with you and your direct manager, and the review is yours to book.

07

Sent back, and never came back

A fact find a manager returns for correction used to fall out of every list — it was no longer waiting on anyone. Eleven were sitting there, the oldest forty days. They are swept every day now.

08

Nothing goes twice

Every email is stamped on the case the moment it leaves. A rerun, a second trigger, a manual re-send — the client still only ever gets it once.

One tap, when it happens

Four taps do the telling.

They are on your approval email, on every chase, and on the page you land on after each tap. The client hears within the minute — in your name.

“Your application is in.”Head office has it. Nothing more for the client to do.

Delivered and paid

Not proceeding

The deep dive — read-only, nothing sent

We asked Salesforce what it already knew.

The audit matched the branch’s own fact finds against the policy records and counted, step by step, how often each date is actually filled in — and by whom.

13

0

11
Sent back, never resubmitted

A step with no date is a step the client is never told about. Not sent late. Never sent. The field exists on every record; it has never once been filled in — so “your policy has been issued” could never have reached anybody, however good the system around it was.

Who does what now

Four cards, one client.

The system sends every client email and chases every stall. These are the few things each of us still does by hand.

🤵
Card 1The advisor
You do
  • Tap Submit with the client’s email on the fact find. The draft goes in your name.
  • Day 8, no answer: call the client when the email asks you to.
  • Four taps, as they happen. The moment it happens, not at the end of the week.
  • Deliver inside 20 days of the policy reaching the branch.
  • Book the yearly review when the client’s answer lands in your inbox.
The system does
  • The draft, the reminders, the milestones, the survey, the yearly review. All in your name, replies to you.
Never
  • Ask a client to print or wet-sign the fact find. They sign online; the signature lands in the client’s box.
🔍
Card 2The direct manager
You do
  • Review and decide within one business day of the client’s Yes.
  • Read the copies. You are copied on every email your advisors’ clients get. Silence in a thread is yours to notice.
  • Act on the chases: nothing keyed at 7 days, undelivered at 10.
  • Anything you send back, follow. It is swept daily now, but the correction is still a conversation.
The system does
  • Holds Agree until the client has confirmed and signed. Tells you the moment they do.
Never
  • Approve before the client’s Yes. Only the branch manager’s link opens that door, and it is recorded.
🗃
Card 3Sales support
You do
  • Key the application the day it comes in, and tell the advisor to tap Signed and submitted if they have not.
  • When a policy arrives at the branch, tell the advisor the same day — their tap starts the delivery clock.
  • Fill in the dates. Dispatch, receipt, delivery. A blank date is a client who never hears from us.
The system does
  • Everything the client hears. Nothing goes from Sales Support’s address.
Never
  • Email a client about their plan. You are not copied, by design.
🛡️
Card 4The branch manager
You do
  • Read every low score personally: advice ratings of 3 or under, and delivery surveys of 3 or under. They come to you alone.
  • Escalations: a policy 15 days at the branch undelivered lands with you.
  • The override, rarely: approve before a client’s Yes, from your own link, with a reason.
  • The morning checks after any change.
The system does
  • Blind-copies you on every client email. Stamps every send. Never sends twice.
Never
  • Show a low rating against an advisor’s name on the wall.
