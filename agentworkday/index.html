5:30am · in your inbox

Your day lands

An e-mail from the branch called Your day. It shows what the team is doing for your clients today: what is overdue, and who is handling it.

  • What the team is working on for you
  • Your book: lapses, arrears, pendings
  • Your goals, week by week
📬 Your day
By 10:30am

Project my day

Tap Project my day. Put a number against each line, then save. Your unit manager sees your projection.

  • Calls, appointments, fact finds
  • Closing interviews and applications
  • Dues and pendings to move
🎯 A number on each line
Any time

Comment on any client

On the same page, comment on any client the team is working on for you. Type it, or tap the microphone and say it. It goes straight onto that task's Chatter, in your name.

  • The team sees it at once
  • No calls back and forth
💬 Straight to Chatter
5:30pm

Close off your day

Put in what you actually did, and 0 where nothing was. The balance against this morning shows as you type. No answer reads as nothing done.

  • Honest numbers, every day
  • Your balance against the morning

One tip

"Sorry, unable to open the file"?

That means your phone is signed in to more than one Google account. It takes ten seconds to get round it:

  1. Press and hold the button
  2. Copy the link
  3. Open it in a private (incognito) window

It works there every time.
