We rise together

This branch took a hit. Look at who is still standing: nineteen agents, two managers and five behind them — twenty-six of us, and two hundred and twenty-one years of this work between us. Ninety-eight agents have come through this branch in the sixteen years the record covers, and in all that time there have been four terminations. We have taken a wave like this before, in 2020, and we were back to where we started inside four years. We led the team that built this company’s first digital application, we have been running on our own screens since 2011, and on 1 August the fact find went live again — rebuilt, with the order put right. On Friday we come back to you: not to brief you, to hear you.
