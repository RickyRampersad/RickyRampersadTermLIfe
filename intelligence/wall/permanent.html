Which is which

  • Econo Life is whole life. The premium is paid to 65 or 85; the cover runs for life. Nothing to convert — it is what a term converts into.
  • Liberator’s premium runs to 65, 75, 85 or 100. Issued 2009 or earlier, it can just be extended. That is the gold figure.
  • Rejuvenator is the critical illness cover. And the riders ride whatever they are attached to, whichever product it is.
  • Every row here is in force and paid to date. A lapsed Liberator cannot be extended, and one in arrears has a premium to collect first.
