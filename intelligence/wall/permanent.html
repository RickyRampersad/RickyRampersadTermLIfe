Which is which

  • The premium paid to 65 is the gold figure. Those are the Liberators that run out of road, and since March 2017 they can be extended for an additional premium.
  • Not free, and not automatic. Full underwriting on the increase only, at the client’s age today — Guardian carries the cost of it, and a rating applies to the increase alone.
  • Three gates. No more than three months in arrears, the original sum assured kept, and never below the company minimum of $100,000.
  • One alteration, and never Rejuvenator. After one premium change it is client accumulation; Rejuvenator cannot be extended this way at all.
