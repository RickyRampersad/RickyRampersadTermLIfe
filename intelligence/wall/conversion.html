What the call is

  • Only term converts. Flexi Term with a C in the code, and nothing else. Liberator and Newlife to sixty-five expire, and the branch book proves it — not one of them has ever been converted.
  • Convertible means no medical. No questions, no evidence of health, and no chance of being declined. Say that first.
  • It is priced at the age they have reached. Before the birthday is cheaper than after it, and that is the whole reason this list is a month.
  • The cover does not grow — the promise does. Same sum assured, for life, instead of ending on a date.
  • The amber tile is a different call, and a harder one. Nothing to exchange, so it is a fresh application and fresh underwriting — and it has to happen before the date, not after it.
