The Branch
Intelligence Wall.

Live on the branch floor. Twenty four hours a day.

Where the money stops

Who crossed the line today, how long they have held cover, how they pay, and whose they are.

At sixty days

We write again — and the letter remembers what they told us the first time.

At ninety

We stop asking them to rate us, and start offering ways to keep the cover.

The contracts we hold

Still in our cabinet, no agent has collected it. That one is ours to fix today.

Collected, not delivered

Every contract an agent is holding carries a name and a number of days. Nothing is anonymous.

Insurance Act 2018 · s268(1)
“In the case of an individual life policy, upon acceptance of the risk, an insurer shall issue a policy within twenty business days of acceptance of the risk.”

And then the Act is silent. It sets no deadline for the days between our cabinet and the client’s hands — so that clock is ours. We set it, and we show it.

The licence year

Life and general, every renewal month on one screen. A licence that lapses is business nobody can write.

Not a picture

Click any bar and the whole wall holds to it — one unit, one agent, one band.
