PENDINGwall

The morning on the pendings

For the support desk, every working morning, before anything else.
Open the pending wall on your phone or the screen. Its first view, Focus today, is the ranked list of cases to move today: why each one, how long it has been outstanding, its last Chatter and by whom, its owner, and the next action. The second, Morning brief, is the seven steps below with today's numbers. Work the focus list from the top; a case is done for the day when its Chatter says what was done.

The seven steps, in order

1
Save it

Guardian closes these within 14 days. Phone the agent and the client today. A case that closes starts again from nothing: a new application, new medicals, a client who has lost patience.

2
Settle it

Approved by underwriting, with only the premium or the last item left. Collect the premium or the last form, submit it, settle today. These are the quickest policies the branch will issue this month.

3
All in

Head office has every item. Ask head office for the decision, on the case, today. Every day a case sits here is head office's, and the wall says so.

4
Answer head office

The underwriter wrote after our last move. Reply on the case and move it the same day. One working day is the deadline; after that the wall marks it late, against the case's owner.

5
Expiring

A medical or a form runs out within 30 days, or already has. Book or redo it before it lapses, or the client goes through it twice.

6
Never ordered

A requirement head office has not ordered. Ask head office to order it. Nobody can deliver a requirement that was never asked for.

7
Chase the agents, one call each

Agent items past three working days. One call per agent with every late item on the list, not one call per item. Post what they said on the case.

How it counts your work

  • A touch is a Pendings task opened, a Chatter post on the case's open task, or an e-mail logged on it, in Salesforce. Closing a task or changing its status is not shown: the wall counts the work that drives a case, and that is the Chatter. Work that is not in Salesforce is not on the wall.
  • Write the note: who, when, how, what was said. A status alone does not tell the branch what was done.
  • The wall reads Salesforce every few minutes and Guardian's report each time it is loaded.

How an agent gets on the wall

  • On the wall lists the agents whose items came in during the last seven days and whose policies settled this week. Deliver, and your name is there.
  • Fastest to submit lists how many days each agent takes from the client signing to the application reaching head office.
  • Agents sticking, on the Accountability view, lists items past three working days, with the codes. Clear them and your name comes off.

First names and the last four digits of a policy only. No client is named anywhere on the wall.
