What you can do there

Check your cover
Every vehicle on the policy, what each is insured for, and the premium.
Set this year’s value
A claim pays the market value or the sum insured, whichever is lower. A figure left too high is premium you can never collect on, so we show you where it should be and you tell us the number.
Fill in what’s missing
Chassis number, driver’s permit and expiry. We can’t issue a certificate without the chassis, and we’d rather not chase you for the permit at claim time.
Take the benefit you’re owed
An individual policyholder is entitled to one of a courtesy car or $15,000 of hospitalisation cover at no extra cost. Choose which.
Send your instruction
It goes to Guardian General with our sales support team copied. Nothing is bound until Guardian confirms your renewal.
