01

The reminder goes out

We send

02

They type their registration number

Client does

The plate is the one motor field that is actually filled in — 225 of 230 records carry it. Spacing and dashes do not matter: pcx-4471 finds PCX 4471.

TT

Looked up against their own policy only. Nobody can type a stranger's plate and see their chassis number, permit or address — the link's access code is the authorisation, and the plate only narrows down what that code can already see.

04

And what stands out

We work out

Worked out live from their own record. Each one has a button that jumps straight to the field that fixes it, so nothing is a dead end.

Needs attention

We do not have your chassis number

It is stamped on your certificate of insurance, and we cannot issue the new one without it.

Needs attention

Insured for the same figure 3 years running

Still TT$180,000. A vehicle is worth less every year, and you cannot be paid more than it is worth — so holding the figure steady usually means paying premium on value that has already gone. Guardian's own pre-agreed rate is 15% a year.

07

They press renew

Client sends

One button. Nothing is bound — it is an instruction, and Guardian confirms.

Send my renewal instruction

The reminder ladder stops for this policy the moment this arrives. A client who has renewed never gets another “your policy renews today”.

09

Why it is worth doing

The point

Last month, across the health book, six reminders went straight to the client and all six paid. Twenty-nine went to an agent and one payment was logged. Same month, same ledger, different inbox.

What goes out

  • The renewal date and the premium
  • What the cover actually gives, from the policy
  • What we are missing
  • What last year cost, and what reached us
  • A free benefit they are entitled to
⇆

What comes back

  • Chassis, engine, permit and expiry
  • A current vehicle value
  • A renewal instruction
  • Benefits they want quoted
  • A current e-mail and mobile

Across 223 motor risks renewing in the next twelve months we are missing the chassis on 53, the driver's permit on 161 and a sum insured on 153. Every renewal worked this way closes some of that — without anyone keying it in.
