Briefing — branch operations email or read out at the huddle 
Subject: Prospecting goes live — what it means for the desk

From today every advisor can send a client a link before their first
appointment. Two things you should know.

WHERE IT LANDS
Everything the client does — opens the page, watches the film, taps what they
want, books — writes to a new tab called *Prospects* in the fact find
workbook. One row per person, not a log. It updates itself.

WHAT ARRIVES IN INBOXES
The advisor is emailed on the first open, again when the client says what they
want, and again when they book. Their unit manager is copied. Nobody needs to
be told by phone.

WHEN AN ADVISOR ASKS YOU
• "How do I send it?" — rickyrampersadbranch.com/prospecting/, type the
  first name, tap Send on WhatsApp.
• "Why is my name not on it?" — they are on the Access tab; check the row
  says Active.
• "The board is empty" — they have not sent one yet. The board only shows
  their own.
• "Nothing arrived" — check their email on the Access tab is right.

WHAT IS NOT LIVE
The fact find changes and the delivery reminders. Do not tell advisors those
are running yet.

NOTHING CHANGES ON YOUR SIDE TODAY
No new form to fill, no report to run. This is the desk knowing what advisors
are being told.

Ricky

After

Week one

What to expect, and what to look at.

Day one
Everybody opens it, nobody sends it Normal. Ask at the huddle who has sent one; the first send is the hard one.
Day two
The first questions arrive Mostly “my name is not on it” and “nothing arrived”. Both are the Access tab — check Active and check the email.
Day three
Somebody picks needs and does not book That is the whole point of the thing. Ring them that day — the board tells the advisor to.
End of week
Look at one number Of the links sent, how many were opened? Below half is a timing problem, not a content one — they are sending at the wrong hour.
