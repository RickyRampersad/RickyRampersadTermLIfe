The flow

Eleven steps

The rail colour says who is holding the file: teal for the advisor and the client, gold for the manager — the one with no stake in the sale.

  1. 1
    Advisor

    Sends the prospect their link

    From the board. Types a first name, taps Send on WhatsApp — the link and the message write themselves, stamped with the advisor’s code and a token unique to that send.

    Goes outrickyrampersadbranch.com/meet/ — personalised
    RecordedSent, against that advisor
  2. 2
    Prospect

    Opens it, and says what they want

    Watches the film, works out their own number in three taps, and taps the needs actually on their mind. Then books — or applies.

    RecordedOpened · watched · what they picked · booked
    Advisor getsAn email the moment they pick, and again when they book
  3. 3
    Advisor and client

    The appointment — the fact find is written live

    Typed while they talk. The figures move as the client answers. No products discussed until the numbers are on the screen.

  4. 4
    Advisor

    Recommends, and writes the reason beside each one

    If a reason cannot be written, the recommendation does not go forward. The addendum picks up every need that did not make the four rows on the form.

  5. 5
    Advisor

    One tap sends it

    Nothing prints, nothing is signed, nothing reaches the client yet.

  6. 6
    Direct manager

    Gets it on his phone — and approves and signs inside the email

    He does not log in anywhere. The email carries the whole case: the client’s numbers, every recommendation, and the reason written against each one. He acts from the message itself, standing up, between appointments.

    From factfind360 · to the direct manager
    Approval needed — Devon Ramkissoon · $2.3m
    1 · Family income “Ten years so they do not have to move.”$1,400,000
    2 · The mortgage “Clears the house outright.”$912,000
    Approve & sign Send back Decline
    Tapping Approve signs it in his name, with the date and time, and writes that signature back into the fact find.

    Send back is not a rejection

    He types one line saying what is missing. It goes to the advisor, the case reopens at the point it failed, and when the advisor fixes it the manager is told it has come back. The client never sees any of it.

  7. 7
    System

    The signature goes into the form

    Not a note in a spreadsheet — the manager’s name, the date and the time are written onto the fact find itself, so the printed pack carries the second signature it is supposed to carry.

    Case stateApproved — released to the client
  8. 8
    Client

    Gets their own copy — and answers from the email

    Plain language. Their needs, what was recommended, and why. Three buttons: agree, ask to discuss it, or decline. Whatever they choose is written back into the fact find, with their reason recorded as carefully as the recommendation was.

    Also capturedTheir rating of the advice, and their signature
  9. 9
    Advisor and client

    Print, wet signature, submit

    Guardian’s six-page form plus the RR Branch addendum. The paperwork comes last, not first.

  10. 10
    System

    Policy delivery, and everybody is told

    Advisor, direct manager and sales support all on the same thread from the moment the client answers through to the policy landing.

  11. 11
    Advisor

    The addendum is the next appointment

    Every need that did not fit on the form, already written down, with the question that opens it.

Five things I need you to decide

1. Can the manager decline outright?

Right now the email offers Approve, Send back, and Decline. Send back is coaching. Decline kills the case. Do you want a manager able to kill a case from a phone, or should everything that is not approved go back to the advisor?

2. How long before a file is chased?

The dash flags anything over a day. Should the system chase the manager automatically at that point, and does that copy anyone above him?

3. Does the client sign before or after the wet signature?

Step 8 captures an in-email signature; step 9 takes a wet one on Guardian’s form. Is the in-email signature a confirmation of the advice, or is it doing more than that? This one has a compliance answer, not a preference answer.

4. Who is sales support on the thread?

One shared address, or a named person per case?

5. Agent codes

Every agent currently sees Premchand’s name and code. I need the real list — name, code, email — before any of this can go branch-wide.
