How a renewal goes

  1. Pick the month. Every Guardian General policy renewing that month is listed from Salesforce, by line. Open one and press Take it.
  2. Last year. What renews, the premium since the policy began, claims, last year's payment, and what the record says. Anyone can help: it is all on one page.
  3. Guardian General's notice. Press Find it in support@: the desk reads the PDF for you. Check the figures, then save. No notice yet? Ask CRMS Renewals for it.
  4. Values. From 45 days before: the client confirms each vehicle's value or the sums insured. Type what they say.
  5. The letter. Draft with AI writes it from the record; read every word and change what you need. Send it for approval.
  6. Approval. The branch manager or the Branch Manager's Assistant approves, and it goes from support@ to the client at least 21 days before the renewal. The renewal task opens on last year's record.
  7. The client's answer. They sign in with a code to their e-mail, see the history since the policy began, and approve, ask for changes, or write a comment. It comes onto the task's Chatter. Reminders go at 14 and 10 days; at 7 days with no answer, call them, and the branch manager decides. Nothing renews without their yes.
  8. Proceed to renew. One press: the new year's billing record and cover terms in Salesforce with the confirmed values, a follow-up task, the instruction to Guardian General's renewals team (support@ and sales support copied), and the delivery in Pawan's calendar.
  9. Guardian General. No reply in two working days: the desk follows up with the trail and posts it on Chatter, every two working days. Their "ready" moves the delivery to the next working day.
  10. Delivered and paid. Mark both; the renewal is done.

Until the branch manager runs girGoLive, the desk is in test mode: letters and instructions go to you and the test inbox, marked TEST, and Salesforce is written only when you tick it.
