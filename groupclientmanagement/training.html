Group client management: the training programme

This is how the Ricky Rampersad Branch looks after its group clients: every week, every month, and every person’s part in it. Read each section and tick it. When all fifteen are ticked, the test opens. Your questions depend on your role.

1The job, and why it matters

Each of your groups is an employer that pays for its employees’ life, health and pension plans with Guardian Life. To that employer, you are Guardian Life.

The branch now runs every group the same way, in two rhythms:

Every week: the service reportEach Monday at 10:00 every group gets one letter: what we completed, what is still open, what we need from them, and the link to their own service page. They answer each item and rate us.
Every month: the billsHead Office’s bills arrive on the 25th. We check every line against Salesforce, answer what does not agree, and send each group its bills and a statement of account at 10:00 on the 26th.

The group’s page has three tabs: Your service items, Your billing and Your people. What they read there is a promise that it is true today. A wrong item, a bill nobody checked, or a date we let slip without a word costs more trust than a late letter ever would.

  • Your groups are the accounts you own in Salesforce. A group moves to someone else only when the account owner is changed in Salesforce.
  • Three places, three jobs. Salesforce is the record. The inboxes are the truth of what was said. The group’s page is what the group reads.
  • Nothing reaches a group that a person has not checked. The system sends at set times, but only what someone approved.

2Who does what

Everyone signs in with their own agent number and password, so everything they do is recorded in their own name. The pages know your role from the Agent Skill Bank.

Branch Manager

Sets the standard and owns the result.

  • Reads the billing report at 13:00 on the 25th, and the daily summary after 10:30.
  • Is told of every late bill and its reason, every rating of 3 or less, and anything about health, claims or the law.
  • The only one who rehearses the runs, switches the bills live, and reconciles a line with a fix still open.
  • Confirms the marks on written test answers.
Branch Manager’s Assistant

Oversees the tasks. Kamla signs in under her own role.

  • Sees and acts on every group: approve, send, comment, write a note for the group. Her approval is logged in her name; the letter is still signed by the person who looks after the group.
  • Every day, reads the Team dashboard and chases what is late: letters not approved by Friday, bills not reconciled by 13:00 on the 25th, answers waiting more than a working day.
  • Steps in when someone is away, so a group never waits on a person who is out.
  • Gives a reason, like everyone else, when she sends a bill after the cut-off.
Support team

Each of you looks after your own groups: the accounts you own in Salesforce.

  • Each week: the six checks, a reason on every item open more than 30 days, then Approve for Monday by Friday.
  • Each month: on the 25th, upload, check, answer, reconcile and approve every bill.
  • Every day: the inboxes, and an answer to every group within one working day.
  • You see every group, so you can help a colleague. A colleague’s group opens read only; you can leave a comment for the team on any item.
The system

Keeps time and keeps the record.

  • Sends the approved letters at 10:00 on Monday, and the approved bills at 10:00 on the billing day.
  • Reminds groups every working day to rate their letter, until they do or write to us.
  • At 10:30 asks anyone whose ready group missed its letter or its bill why, and opens a task for the reason.
  • Chases each change form every three working days, and logs every step (section 12).

3Your week: the service report

Monday10:00: the letters go by themselves. Every letter that was approved is written again from Salesforce and sent from support@, with sales support copied. A letter that waited (a new item since you checked, a reason missing, the group no longer ready) comes back to you by e-mail with the reason: put it right and approve it again, and it goes in the next hourly run.
10:30: the group day. Each group that had a letter and has not rated gets a reminder, every working day, until it rates or writes to us. If one of your ready groups had no letter this week, you get an e-mail asking why, and a Salesforce task opens in your name: GROUP LETTER NOT SENT … reason needed. Reply by 12 noon, put it right today, and close the task with the reason. The reason is a KPI.
Tue – WedWork the open items. Keep Salesforce current as you go: the status, the target dates, Chatter, and e-mails filed on the record.
ThursdayGroups answer by Friday. Each answer reaches you by e-mail, and you act on it by the next working day (section 11). Start the six checks (section 4) on each of your groups.
FridayApprove. Finish the checks, deal with every escalation (section 5), write the reasons (section 10), then preview and approve each group marked Approve for Monday. You can approve until 10:00 on Monday, but Friday leaves Monday morning for what changed. An approval made after 10:00 on Monday is for the next Monday.
Every dayRead support@ and your own inbox. Nothing from a group waits more than one working day for at least an acknowledgement.
On the group’s page they mark each item Correct, Needs a change or Not ours, and rate us from one to five stars. An answer of Needs a change or Not ours stays first on your To act on list for 14 days, until the item is closed or a note for the group follows it.

4Before you approve: the six checks

The letter is written again from Salesforce at ten on Monday, and an item that appears after you approve holds it. These checks make Salesforce true first.

  1. Open the group and read it. Read the chips: Approve for Monday, waiting on them, past target, needs a reason. Read Letter goes to: is it the right person? (Personal mailboxes: section 9.)
  2. Check every open Salesforce task. Is it still open? Is the status right? Is the target date real? Is the right person on it? Close what is done. If a date has passed and you cannot finish today, set a realistic new date with a Chatter note saying why. Never move a date just to clear the red: every move is recorded, and the group sees the first target date beside the current one.
  3. Scrub the inboxes. Search support@, your own Outlook and the sales support inbox for the group’s name, its list bill, the contact’s address and each item’s subject. Look for: an e-mail nobody answered (answer it first), a document or form they sent (update the item), a payment confirmation (check the billing), a complaint (section 5). The inbox is the truth: file what matters on the record.
  4. See what the group actually has. In Salesforce, open the account: its policies and list bill, the latest billing record, who is joining or leaving. A letter that contradicts the group’s own billing or headcount tells them we do not know their business.
  5. Update, then refresh. Salesforce first: statuses, dates, Chatter notes, e-mails filed. Then tap Refresh from Salesforce, so the board and the letter read the new state.
  6. Preview, check, approve. Write the reason for any item open more than 30 days. Preview: the right contact and greeting, the group’s items and nothing else, your opening line if you want one. Tick I have checked every item against Salesforce and the latest billing, then Approve for Monday. If something changes before Monday, approve it again.
Untick an item only when it is not the group’s to see (section 9). Never untick to hide a problem. If it is true and it is theirs, they see it.

7Billing, step by step

Open Billing on a group (or Group billing in the branch app). One group, one month, eight steps. Each step shows a count, and the bar fills only when a step is really done.

1
Upload the bills

Each line takes its own documents:

  • Group Health: Guardian’s invoice (PDF). Under it, the claims experience if you have it.
  • Group Life: the member bill, its Statement of Adjustment and the covering letter.
  • Pension: the list bill (a spreadsheet), or built from the policies carrying the list bill in Salesforce.

The bill is read on your device. Check the month at the top before you go on. The claims experience is added up on your device by month, so no patient’s name reaches the sheet.

2
What was found

Every check against Salesforce is a finding, with a level and whose it is:

Fix wrong: put it rightCheck may be wrong: lookNote worth knowingOK agrees

The checks: the month’s billing record and its amount; the arrears on the bill against the payments in Salesforce; every member against the group’s policies; who joined, left or changed tier since last month; each back-charge and adjustment against a task on file; the life rates and the 31-day rule; the list bill against the policies tagged with it; payments keyed long after they were made. Whose it is says where the fix belongs: Salesforce, Head Office, the group or us. The AI’s read is for you, never the group.

3
Answer the questions

Every open finding becomes a question, with answers to tap. Your answer goes on the bill, on Group Changes and on the line’s billing task’s Chatter in your name. An answer that settles it resolves the finding; some open the next step for you: upload again, set the Salesforce figure, record a payment, open a task, or send a termination form. Not sure yet is always there, and leaves the finding open.

4
Last month’s tasks

Every open billing task is read against this month’s bill: settled, still open or worse, with an update drafted from its history. Read it, correct it, then Post: the task’s owner is mentioned and e-mailed. Tick close it only when the bill shows it is done.

5
Terminations and changes

The joiners, leavers and tier changes the bills show, with Record a change and the three Guardian forms (section 8).

6
The people

Everyone on the bills matched to Salesforce: turning 65 within six months (the group cover ends, and they can convert), a dependant coming of age, retiring on the company pension, still billed past the age, a family tier with nobody on record, a single tier with dependants, no date of birth, no Salesforce record. For each, a letter to the member (the facts and the offer of a call from a licensed agent, never advice), a task, or the enrolment form. The group sees counts only.

7
The statement

One page: this month’s lines, the earlier invoices Salesforce still shows unpaid, Head Office’s own arrears where they differ, and the total to bring the account up to date, with the changes on this billing. It goes with the bills, and the group sees it on their page.

8
Approve and send

Reconciled is your tick that you checked the line against Salesforce yourself, and it is refused while a fix is open (only the Branch Manager may override). Then Approve for the billing day: the PDFs are kept safe in Drive until the run. Cancel the approval takes it back. Send now is for a bill that cannot wait.

Never: tick Reconciled to get a bill out; send a bill that still bills a leaver without the correction raised; put a member’s health or claim in a note; or press Open a task, close it or a fix just to see what happens. Every press writes to Salesforce.

9What the group sees, and what it never sees

Your service itemsThe weekly items, their history and target dates, the year so far, the pending applications and increases, and what is coming up for their people.
Your billingThe statement of account, each line’s latest bill, twelve months billed and received, the open billing items, and for health what the plan paid by year.
Your peopleWho is pending, active, or came off this year. A message on a person goes to that task’s Chatter and the person who has it is e-mailed; with nothing open, a task opens for the account owner.
  • Shown unless you untick: items on the group’s own account, its billing records, or naming the group.
  • Hidden unless you tick: items reached only through an employee’s own policy or contact. Tick one only when it is plan administration the employer handles, such as someone joining or leaving.
  • Never, whatever is ticked: a member’s health or a claim, a date of birth, an e-mail, a phone number, a beneficiary, a loss ratio, the AI’s read, or a Chatter comment that was not written for the group. If HR asks about a member’s health, explain that it is between the member and Guardian Life, and that we deal with the member directly.
  • The access code. Every letter and bill carries the group’s list bill and access code, so it goes only to the Contact Person in Salesforce. If that is a personal mailbox, confirm it before you approve. Never forward a letter, and never send it to another employee to pass on.

10Writing to the group

The note under an item is plain facts, a date that is in Salesforce, and what happens next, in one or two sentences.

Good “Your September leavers went for processing on 1 October. We expect the corrected bill by 16 October.”
Never blame (“head office has been slow again”), internal names or departments, words copied from Chatter, a date you cannot keep, or anything about a member’s health.
  • Items open more than 30 days need a note shared in the last 30 days before the letter can go. The page marks each one.
  • Draft with AI writes a first draft from the item’s history. Read it, correct anything that does not match Salesforce, then Post. Nothing is shared until you press Post, and once you do, it is yours.
  • Your opening line is optional: a thank-you, or a heads-up about something important this week. It is never the place for internal problems.
  • On the phone the same rules hold: own it, give plain facts and a date, then do what you said.

11Follow-up: after the letter and the bill

  • Answers come in by Friday. Each one reaches you and sales support by e-mail, and any note the group writes goes on the task’s Chatter.
  • Act by the next working day. Needs a change: fix it or find out why, tell the group what you are doing, note it in Chatter. Not ours: find out whose it is, correct Salesforce, tell the group. A rating of 3 or less: call the contact within two working days, and tell the Branch Manager.
  • No rating? The system reminds the group every working day. A group that writes to support@ instead is owed a person’s reply, and the reminders stop. If they still have not answered by Friday, call them before you approve the next letter: check they got it and can sign in, and note it in Chatter. Never answer for the group.
  • Billing questions and messages on Your people are answered within a working day, on the task, where the group can see the note you share.
  • Keep your promises. When you give a group a date, put it in Salesforce. If it is going to slip, tell the group before the date, with the new date and what you are doing. Then confirm when it is done.
  • Close the loop. An item is done when the group’s problem is solved and they know it, not when the task is closed.

13Taking initiative

Initiative is fixing the cause, not just the item, and doing it before anyone asks.

  • See the pattern. The same item stuck on three groups is one problem. Raise it once with the details, copy the BMA, set a follow-up date, and tell each group what you are doing.
  • Don’t wait for a colleague. If a group is waiting on someone who is away, leave a comment for the team on the item and ask the BMA to act on it or move it.
  • Fix records at the source. A contact who left, a wrong list bill, a missing e-mail, a member with no record: correct it in Salesforce, not around it.
  • Ask before the deadline. If Head Office needs something from the group, ask early, not on the day it is due. A bill question found on the 25th is answered on the 25th.
  • Bring a next step. When you cannot solve it today, tell the group what happens next and when.
  • Tell the managers what you find: a gap in a process, a group at risk, a better way of doing it.

14Practice: questions with answers

Answer each one in your head, then open it. These are not the test questions.

I approve a group’s bill at 9:40 on the billing day. When does it go?

At 10:00 that morning, by itself, with its PDFs and the statement. You have not missed the cut-off, so no reason is asked.

The 26th is a Saturday. When do the bills go, and when is the report?

The bills go at 10:00 on Monday 28th (or the next working day, if Monday is a holiday). The report goes at 13:00 on Friday the 25th.

I approve a bill at 11:15 on the billing day. What happens?

The page asks why it was not done by 10:00. Give the real reason. It then goes at 10:00 the next working day, unless you use Send now. Either way the reason is recorded and the Branch Manager sees it.

Why is there no Send now button on my bill?

Send now only appears once the bill is approved. Reconcile it, approve it, and Send now is there.

A finding says “fix · Head Office”. Is it mine?

You raise it. Whose it is says where the correction is made, not who follows it up. Raise it with Head Office with the facts, answer the question so it is on the record, and tell the group it is in hand if it touches their bill.

Can I tick Reconciled with a fix still open, so the bill catches the run?

No. The page refuses it, and only the Branch Manager may override. A bill that goes on time but wrong costs more than a bill that goes a day late with a reason.

Kamla approved my group’s letter while I was out. Whose name is on it?

Yours: the letter is signed by whoever looks after the group. The approval is recorded in Kamla’s name.

A colleague is away and her group’s HR officer has been waiting two days for an answer. Her group is read only for me. What do I do?

Acknowledge HR today, leave a comment for the team on the item saying what you told them, and ask the BMA to act on the group or move it to you.

My ready group had no letter this week. What do I get, and what do I do?

At 10:30 on Monday, an e-mail asking why and a task in your name, GROUP LETTER NOT SENT … reason needed. Reply by noon with the real reason, put it right today, and close the task with the reason.

A group’s HR writes that an employee left on 30 September. What do I send?

EB0010, from the page, for the termination. Search the member, give the effective date, preview, and send. It goes to EB Customer Care with sales support and support@ copied, and the owner’s task follows it up.

What can the group see about a member who turns 65 in four months?

That someone on their plan is coming up to 65, as a count on their billing and their name under Coming up. The member gets the letter. Never their date of birth.

Is anything written anywhere while the bills are in test mode?

Yes. Bills and forms go only to you and the branch, marked TEST, but Salesforce is real: a Reconciled tick, a fix, a Chatter post and a task are written for real. Press only what you mean.

HR asks on Your people why a member’s claim was reduced. What do I say?

That a member’s claims stay between the member and Guardian Life, and that we will speak to the member directly. Nothing about the claim goes to the employer.

Head Office asks who sent a group’s bill late in October, and why. Where is it?

On the bill’s row: Approved by, Sent by and the late reason. The same is on Group Changes and the billing task’s Chatter, and in the Branch Manager’s summary for that day.

15How you are appraised, and the test

The test is weighted like your appraisal. Your questions depend on your role: the support team take the questions on the work; the Branch Manager’s Assistant and the Branch Manager take the questions on oversight; everyone takes confidentiality, client communication and the audit trail. Your weights:

  • To pass: 80% overall, every critical question right, and no competency under 60%. The critical questions are on confidentiality and on what a group may see.
  • Each competency is rated Exceeds (90% and over), Meets (75–89%), Developing (60–74%) or Not yet (under 60%).
  • Written answers are marked by AI against a marking guide, and the Branch Manager confirms the mark. Write them the way you would do the work: the steps, in order, and why.
  • Your result is logged with feedback on every question, and e-mailed to you and the Branch Manager. You may take the test again; every attempt is kept.
  • On the job the same competencies show on the board: letters approved by Friday, bills reconciled by 13:00 on the 25th, reasons given the first time, items past target, ratings, and how quickly answers are followed up.
