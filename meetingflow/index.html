Four parts of a branch meeting

Forty-seven seconds. What is expected before we meet, at the start, through the middle, and after it.

BeforeYou bring one thing. Your agent number opens the page, you pick the item and answer its question. It is on the agenda before anybody sits down.
The startPress I am here. That is the register — nothing else marks you present. Then ten minutes of quiet while the room reads.
The middleFifty minutes. Every question gets an answer, or a name and a date on it.
AfterTwenty minutes of decisions, read back out loud. The minutes come within the hour and it is on your board by Monday.
There are two taps, not one. Your agent number opens the page. The I am here button on the meeting is what writes your attendance row. Sign in and never press it, and the register has you absent.
