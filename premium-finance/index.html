Questions people ask
Straight answers, before you apply.
What is premium financing?

A short loan that pays your insurance premium. We pay your insurer the full premium, and you repay us in equal monthly instalments. The policy is yours, in force from its start date, exactly as if you had paid it yourself.

Why is a shorter plan cheaper?

Interest is charged each month only on what you still owe. A shorter plan pays the balance down faster, so there's less owing each month and less interest overall.

What does 2% a month come to over a year?

2% a month is 24% a year nominal, or about 26.8% a year effective. But you only borrow for 6 to 10 months, and on a falling balance, so what you actually pay is the cost of financing on your quote, and nothing else.

Can I pay it off early?

Yes. Because interest runs on the balance, paying early means you pay interest only up to the day you settle. Ask us for a settlement figure.

What do I need to apply?

Your ID, and the insurer's quotation, invoice or renewal notice for the policy. We'll ask for copies after you submit.

Which policies can be financed?

Motor, property, marine, liability and most general insurance, and some life and health premiums, from any insurer in Trinidad and Tobago. We confirm with your insurer that the policy can be financed and that unused premium is refundable on cancellation.
