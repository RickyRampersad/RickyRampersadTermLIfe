The Insurance Act · Trinidad and Tobago

“An ordinary policy shall not be forfeited by reason only of non-payment of any premiums where the surrender value of the policy, calculated as at the day immediately preceding that on which the premium falls due, exceeds the sum of the amount of the debts owing to the insurer under or secured by the policy and the amount of the overdue premium.”
A policy that had built a value does not end the day a premium is missed: the Act keeps it in force from that value first. That is why a policy that stopped being paid can still hold something, and why we would like to check yours, free. Everything else the law gives you →
