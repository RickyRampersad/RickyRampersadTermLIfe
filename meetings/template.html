Every branch meeting from now on runs to the same shape. Wednesday, 9:00 to 10:30, the same eleven slots in the same order. You know before you walk in what will be asked of you, and the chair does not build the morning out of whatever people brought.

Built from the branch's own meeting record, March to September 2026, and the Salesforce roster as at 9 October 2026. The four stages of how a meeting is run — before, during, after, follow-up — are on the framework page.

The shape of every Wednesday

Eleven slots, ninety minutes, 9:00 to 10:30. The chair holds 15 of the 90 minutes. Everything else belongs to somebody else by name, and sixteen of those minutes belong to the whole room rather than to anybody on the agenda.

10:30 is a finish time, not an ambition. The branch ran 113 minutes on 7 August, 82 on 21 August and 54 on 11 September — three meetings, three different lengths, none of them planned. Ninety minutes is now the shape, and if an item needs more than its minutes it comes out of the meeting and goes to a named pair who report back the following week.

What fills the 9:59 slot

One standing slot, cycling, so these subjects get a hearing without a fixed agenda item that is empty three weeks in four. Every one of them is lifted from the branch's own role KPI lists — they are what people are already measured on, not a syllabus invented for the meeting.

Week 1 — Production and quota

Where every unit stands against quota, off the producer board on the Fact Find wall — need found, cover recommended, cover sold, API picked up. The one number the branch is actually measured on.

Week 2 — Persistency and conservation

Two-year and five-year, lapse activity and what was saved. Named, with what was done about it.

Week 3 — Recruitment and licensing

Every recruit by name against licensing, and the Central Bank renewals by month.

Week 4 — Goal plans and one-to-ones

Who has filed an FY27 Flight Plan and where they stand against their own API goal. Quarterly this becomes the appraisal review.

The five walls →

Everybody speaks, and the arithmetic says so

The branch meeting has been one person talking for most of an hour. That is what this template is built to end, and it is enforced by the clock rather than by goodwill.

The chair's share

17%15 minutes of 90. If the chair is presenting more than that, an item has lost its owner.

Named presenters

6slots a week carry somebody else's name. Two are unit heads, two rotate through the whole roster.

Everyone in the room

16minutes belong to the room — the open floor and one client each. Nobody leaves having said nothing.

The rotation is held by the app, not by memory. The two rotating slots go to whoever is next on the rota and has not presented most recently. Nobody is asked twice before everybody has been asked once, and nobody can quietly never be asked.

The register is the login

  1. You sign in to see the meeting. rickyrampersadbranch.com/meetings — your agent number and your password. Check-in opens 30 minutes before the start.
  2. Signing in is the register. There is no sheet going round and no form to fill. Signing in after 9:00 is recorded as late, which is not the same as absent.
  3. If you cannot come, sign in anyway and pick your reason. You will not get the meeting, but the wall shows you as an apology with the reason beside it rather than as an absence. Do it as soon as you know.
  4. No login is absent. Not a judgement — just what the record says when nothing was entered. It takes about eight seconds to not be that.

The flow around the hour

The meeting is one of four stages. The detail of who does what at each — chair, presenter, sales support, agent, guest — is on the framework page.

  1. Before — the meeting is built, not assembled in the room. The chair publishes the agenda with a clock against every item and a name against every item. Presenters upload and mark Ready. Materials close at 5pm the day before.
  2. During — the hour itself, against the clock. Check-in opens at 8:30. Eleven slots, 9:00 to 10:30. An item that overruns leaves the room with a named pair.
  3. After — the record, within two working days. Every action carries a name and a date. The minutes go out whether or not anybody asks for them.
  4. Follow-up — until every action is closed. This is the stage the branch has never had. 46 actions were raised across five meetings and one was closed.
