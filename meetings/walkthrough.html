Our meeting has changed. It is no longer one person at the front for an hour and a half — it is a working meeting, and everybody in the room contributes. Watch this once and you will know how to sign in, what you will be asked, and what your own two minutes are for.

1 minute 33· Sound on — it is narrated, and captioned either way

What to do, in four steps

  1. Go to the meeting rickyrampersadbranch.com/meetings — from your phone is fine. Check-in opens at 8:30.
  2. Sign in with your agent number and password The ones you already have from the branch portfolio. Nothing new to remember. Signing in is the register — there is no sheet going round.
  3. Read the agenda before Wednesday Every item has a name against it. If one of them is yours, it is already on your Prepare screen — upload what you are presenting and mark it ready.
  4. Bring your one client At 10:17 every person in the room names one client they are calling before Friday. That is the contribution, and it is about twenty seconds.
